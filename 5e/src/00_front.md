# Mutagen 5e

*The Mutant: a fifth-edition class for people whose bodies are rewriting themselves. Five Forms, a push-your-luck Surge engine, a Mutation menu, Regeneration from 1st level, and a bestiary from the flooded city of Halcyon.*

> ##### What's in This Book
> - **The Mutant** (class, levels 1–20) with five subclasses, the **Forms**: Juggernaut, Skitter, Oracle, Alembic and Siren.
> - **Surge Parts**, the **Mutation Trees** and the **Overload** tables.
> - **Options:** the GRAFT Freelancer background, five feats and Mutagen gear.
> - **Bestiary:** twenty stat blocks, CR ½ to 23, including three Bosses with legendary actions.
> - **Design Notes:** how the original *Mutagen* rules map to 5e, and the balance targets the class was tuned to.
>
> You need the fifth-edition core rules; everything this book relies on is in the **System Reference Document 5.1**, a full copy of which ships alongside it (`5e/srd/`).

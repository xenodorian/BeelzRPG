# BeelzRPG — MUTAGEN

![Mutagen cover](art/cover.jpg)

**Mutagen** is a compact tabletop RPG of body-horror, chrome, and bad decisions in the quarantined megacity of Halcyon. No races, no classes: you build your freelancer from **Augment trees** grown as flesh (**Bio**) or bolted on as metal (**Chrome**). In combat, your body **Surges**: dice pick a few powers from everything you've become, and every one you use pushes you toward **Overload**.

- **One roll:** d20 + Stat (+ Rank) vs a target number. Players roll both attacks *and* defenses.
- **Emotive stats:** **Grit, Moxie, Nerve, Gumption, Chutzpah.**
- **Six skill trees:** Hide, Limbs, Senses, Glands, Wiring, Mind. Each node scales with level.
- **Surges:** build powers from **Shape + Effect + Twist** parts; your **Sequence die** decides which are ready each fight.
- **Flux & Overload:** power has a price, rolled on the Bloom or Glitch tables.
- **10 missions, 10 levels.**

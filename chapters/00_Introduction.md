# 0. Introduction — Welcome to Halcyon

![The Spill at Halcyon](../art/setting_halcyon.jpg)

**Mutagen** is a tabletop roleplaying game about freelancers who rebuild their own bodies to survive a city that is rebuilding itself. There are no races and no classes. You are whatever you have grafted on: chitin and venom, chrome and wire, or a twitching mix of both. In a fight your body does not always do what you planned; it **Surges**, offering you a random handful of powers from everything you have ever become.

## What You Need
- 3–5 players and one Game Master (GM).
- A d20 per player, plus a few d4, d6, d8, d10, and d12.
- A character sheet per player ([sheets/](../sheets/)).
- Tokens for **Juice** (coins, beads; 3 per player).

## The Setting in One Page
Thirty years ago the **Ostrander Biotech Arcology**, a mile-high lab tower in the coastal megacity of **Halcyon**, cracked open. Millions of litres of **Mutagen**, a programmable living gel, poured into the water table. We call it **the Spill**.

Mutagen does one thing: it makes flesh *accept change*. A dose lets your body grow a new organ, or lets it accept a steel arm without rejection. Untamed, it makes rats fuse into hulks, mould think, and machines grow meat. The national government walled Halcyon off and outsourced the problem.

**G.R.A.F.T.** (Genetic Response And Field Tactics) is the private contractor that won the cleanup bid. GRAFT does not hire soldiers. It licenses **freelancers**: people already saturated with Mutagen who can go where clean humans die. You are one of them. GRAFT pays per mission, sells you upgrades at its clinics, and owns everything you bring back.

**Factions in a sentence each:**
- **GRAFT** — your employer. Polite, underfunded, and lying about what's in the samples.
- **Ostrander Remnant** — the old company, still alive in the upper arcology, sending sterile "Cleaner" squads to erase evidence.
- **The Children of the Bloom** — a faith that believes the Spill is the next step of evolution, and that you are halfway there.
- **The Scrapjacks** — chrome scavengers who strip the dead (and the living) for parts.
- **The Drowned Wards** — ordinary people living in the flooded lower city, trying not to become anything.

## Tone
Gritty, weird, and a little funny. Bodies are strange and that is not tragic by default; it can be glorious, gross, or both. Missions are short and dangerous. The city is enormous and mostly unexplored.

## The Big Ideas
1. **One roll for everything:** d20 + Stat (+ Rank) against a number. ([Chapter 1](01_Core_Rules.md))
2. **Emotive stats:** Grit, Moxie, Nerve, Gumption, Chutzpah. They describe *how* you do things, not what you are. ([Chapter 2](02_Character_Creation.md))
3. **You are your Augments:** six skill trees (Hide, Limbs, Senses, Glands, Wiring, Mind), each grown as **Bio** or installed as **Chrome**. ([Chapter 3](03_Augment_Trees.md))
4. **Surges:** you build a personal list of combat powers from mix-and-match parts. Each fight, dice decide which few are ready. Using them is optional; your weapons and Augments always work. ([Chapter 4](04_Surges.md))
5. **Flux:** every Surge pushes your body closer to **Overload**, where the Mutagen takes the wheel. ([Chapter 5](05_Flux_and_Overload.md))
6. **Player-facing dice:** the GM rarely rolls. Players roll to hit, and players roll to dodge. ([Chapter 6](06_Combat.md))
7. **One mission, one level:** a campaign runs 10 missions, levels 1 to 10. ([Chapter 7](07_Advancement.md))

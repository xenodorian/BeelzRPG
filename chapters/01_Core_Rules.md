# 1. Core Rules

Mutagen uses one roll for everything.

## The Roll
> **Roll d20 + Stat (+ Rank, if it applies). Meet or beat the target number to succeed.**

- **Stat** is one of your five stats: −1 to +3 at level 1, rising to as high as **+8** as your Base Form Evolves ([Chapter 2A](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md)), +10 in Final Form.
- **Rank** is your level-based bonus: **Rank = half your level, rounded up** (1 at level 1–2, up to 5 at level 9–10).
- You **always** add Rank to **attack rolls** and **defense rolls**.
- You add Rank to a **check** only if one of your **Knacks** applies.
- Ties go to the player.

There are three kinds of roll:

| Roll | When | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Check** | You try something risky outside of attacking or defending | A **DC** set by the GM |
| **Attack** | You try to hurt something | The target's **Guard** |
| **Defense** | Something tries to hurt you | The attacker's **Threat Number (TN)** |

The GM almost never rolls. Enemies don't roll to hit you; **you roll to avoid them** (see [Combat](06_Combat.md)).

## Difficulty (DC)

| DC | Difficulty | Example |
|---|---|---|
| — | Trivial | No roll. Just do it. |
| 10 | Easy | Climb a chain-link fence in the rain |
| 13 | Standard | Talk a Ward guard into letting you pass |
| 16 | Hard | Hotwire an Ostrander security door |
| 19 | Extreme | Leap between two moving maglev cars |
| 22 | Mutant | Hold a collapsing ceiling up with your bare (augmented) back |
| 25 | Monstrous | Outrun a maglev; shout down a riot; read a stranger's mind from across a plaza |
| 28 | Mythic | Only an Evolved freelancer's primary stat even has a chance |

Only roll when failure is interesting. On a failure, the GM picks a consequence: you fail, you succeed at a cost, or the situation gets worse.

**DCs don't scale with level.** A locked door is DC 16 whether you're level 1 or 9. Evolved freelancers simply *are* better at things their Form is good at: by level 7, a primary stat of +8 passes a Hard check on an 8+. Raise the stakes instead of the DC: higher-level missions put Extreme and Mutant obstacles in your path, and ask for checks with your weak stats. See [Running the Game](09_Running_the_Game.md#difficulty-across-the-campaign).

## Knacks
A **Knack** is a short, specific phrase describing something you're trained in: *Street Medicine*, *Lying to Cops*, *Old Ostrander Tech*, *Parkour*, *Sewer Navigation*. When a Knack fits a check, add your Rank. If it's arguable, the GM decides; lean toward yes for narrow Knacks. You start with 3 Knacks and gain more as you level.

## Advantage and Disadvantage
**Advantage:** roll 2d20, keep the higher. **Disadvantage:** roll 2d20, keep the lower. They don't stack; if you have both, roll normally.

**Player-facing rule:** if an *enemy* would have advantage on an attack, the defending player rolls their defense with **disadvantage**, and vice versa.

## Natural 20s and 1s
- **Natural 20 on an attack:** a **Crit**. Roll the damage dice twice.
- **Natural 20 on a defense:** you avoid it entirely, and the attacker is **Exposed** (see [Conditions](06_Combat.md#conditions)).
- **Natural 20 on a check:** success, plus a bonus the GM describes.
- **Natural 1 on anything:** automatic failure.

## Group Checks
When the whole party attempts something together (sneaking past, hauling a door), everyone rolls. If at least half succeed, the group succeeds.

## Helping
To help someone, describe how and use your Action (in combat) or just do it (outside combat). They roll with advantage.

## Juice
**Juice** is a reward token for great play: clever plans, bold roleplay, making the table laugh, or suffering for the story. The GM hands it out.
- You start each session with **1 Juice** and can hold **3**.
- Spend 1 Juice to do **one** of:
  - **Reroll** any d20 you just rolled (use the new result).
  - **Choose a Prime:** set one of your Sequence dice to any result you want (see [Surges](04_Surges.md)).
  - **Shrug:** end one condition on yourself.

## Stats at a Glance

| Stat | It's about | Rolls it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Grit** | Toughness, force, refusal to fall over | Power melee, lifting, breaking, **Endure** defense, Max HP |
| **Moxie** | Daring, speed, flair under pressure | Finesse melee, sprinting, sneaking, climbing, **Dodge** defense, initiative |
| **Nerve** | Cool head, steady hands, willpower | Ranged attacks, noticing things, **Resist** defense, Flux Limit |
| **Gumption** | Resourcefulness, know-how, figuring it out | Tech, medicine, science, hacking, Analyze, Patch |
| **Chutzpah** | Audacity, charm, nerve-in-your-face | Persuade, lie, intimidate, perform, Taunt, Rally, psionic Surges |

## Rest
- **Breather** (about an hour, somewhere quiet): regain **half your Max HP**, Flux drops to **0**. You can take **two** Breathers between Lockdowns.
- **Lockdown** (8 hours in a safe place): regain all HP, Flux to 0, end temporary Overload effects, reset Breathers and "once per Lockdown" abilities.

## Time and Distance
Combat runs in **rounds** (about 6 seconds). Distances are in feet. Play them either way ([Positioning](06_Combat.md#positioning-theatre-of-the-mind-or-grid)): in **theatre of the mind** use **Close** (5 ft, touching), **Near** (up to 30 ft, one move), **Far** (up to 120 ft) and **Distant** (beyond); on a **grid**, 1 square = 5 ft and diagonals count as one square.

## Golden Rules
1. **Specific beats general.** An Augment or Surge that says otherwise wins.
2. **Round down** unless told otherwise (minimum 1 for damage).
3. **The GM decides** when the rules are unclear, then writes it down and moves on.

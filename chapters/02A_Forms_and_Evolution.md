# 2A. Base Forms & Evolution

![A Bio freelancer mid-change](../art/char_bio.jpg)

The Mutagen doesn't make you *anything*. It makes you into **something**: one of five shapes the city's freelancers keep turning into. That shape is your **Base Form**. It sets your best stats and the three Augment trees you grow along. Twice in your career it **Evolves**, suddenly and violently, into something bigger.

## Choosing a Form

| Form | Primary +3 | Secondary +2 | Tertiary +1 | You place +0 and −1 in | Primary tree | Secondary | Tertiary | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [**Juggernaut**](#juggernaut) | Grit | Nerve | Chutzpah | Moxie, Gumption | Hide | Limbs | Glands | *"I don't move. You do."* |
| [**Skitter**](#skitter) | Moxie | Gumption | Grit | Nerve, Chutzpah | Wiring | Limbs | Senses | *"You blinked. I was already there."* |
| [**Oracle**](#oracle) | Nerve | Chutzpah | Moxie | Grit, Gumption | Senses | Mind | Wiring | *"I saw you coming. Three days ago."* |
| [**Alembic**](#alembic) | Gumption | Grit | Nerve | Moxie, Chutzpah | Glands | Hide | Mind | *"Hold still. This will either cure you or kill it."* |
| [**Siren**](#siren) | Chutzpah | Moxie | Gumption | Grit, Nerve | Mind | Wiring | Limbs | *"Everybody look at me."* |

- **Stats:** your Form fixes your +3, +2 and +1. You put **+0** in one of the other two stats and **−1** in the last.
- **Trees:** you take nodes only from your Form's three trees ([chapter 3](03_Augment_Trees.md)): 3 in the primary, 2 in the secondary and 1 in the tertiary at level 1.
- **Material:** for each tree, choose **Bio or Chrome**, as before. A Chrome Oracle and a Bio Oracle both see everything, but one does it with lenses and the other with eyes.
- **Every stat** is primary for one Form, secondary for one and tertiary for one.

## Stages

| Stage | Levels | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary | Dump (+0) | Dump (−1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base** | 1–3 | +3 | +2 | +1 | +0 | −1 |
| **Evolution I** | 4–6 | **+5** | +3 | +2 | +1 | +0 |
| **Evolution II** | 7–10 | **+8** | +4 | +3 | +2 | +1 |
| **Final Form** (active) | 10 | **+10** | +4 | +3 | +2 | +1 |

There are no other stat increases. Your stats only change when your body does.

## What an Evolution Gives
**Evolution I** happens at **level 4** and **Evolution II** at **level 7**. Each one gives you all of the following:

| | Evolution I (level 4) | Evolution II (level 7) |
|---|---|---|
| **Stats** | +2 primary, +1 every other stat | +3 primary, +1 every other stat |
| **Prime** | 2 → **3** | 3 → **4** |
| **Sequence die** | d6 → **d8** | d8 → **d12** (skips d10) |
| **Signature Surge** | Your **first** Signature Surge | Your **second** Signature Surge |
| **Evolution node** | Your Form's level 4 node | Your Form's level 7 node |
| **Look** | Your Form's Evolution I look | Your Form's Evolution II look |

Your Max HP, defenses, Flux Limit and attacks all rise with the new stats. **Right after an Evolution, fights get noticeably easier.** The threats of the next few missions catch up with you (see [Threats](10_Threats.md)).

### Signature Surges
A Signature Surge is the power your new body was built around, and it's always there. You build it from your Surge parts like any Surge, with the Twists your Rank allows, and name it after what your body does now. It sits in its own Signature box (not on your Sequence), it's Primed at the start of every combat and again after each use, so you can Unleash it **once per round**, every round, for its normal Flux. The full rules are in [Signature Surges](04_Surges.md#signature-surges).
- **Flux is the limit.** You can now Surge every turn, but every Surge pushes you toward Overload. Pace yourself, or plan your Overloads.

### Emergence: the moment it happens
You gain the level, and its numbers, at the debrief like any other level. The **metamorphosis plays out on screen**. At the start of your first turn in the **first combat** of your next mission, you **Emerge**. If you and the GM agree on an earlier moment of terror (a fall, a fire, a friend going Down), it happens then instead.
1. **Describe it.** Use your Form's look below: what splits, what grows, what you hear.
2. **Gain temporary HP equal to 2 × your level.**
3. **Every enemy within 30 ft** that can see you is **Shaken** (Bosses ignore this).
4. The **first time** you Unleash your new Signature Surge this combat, it's **Amped** (0 Flux).

Emerging takes no action. It happens once per Evolution.

> **GM tip.** Before the mission, ask the player: *"What's the first sign it's happening?"* Plant it in the Approach scene: an itch under the plating, a second heartbeat, a voice in the wiring. Optional downtime **Chrysalis** scene: see [Running the Game](09_Running_the_Game.md#evolution-at-the-table).

### Final Form (level 10)
Your third and last stage. At level 10 you take your Form's **Final look** for good. Once per Lockdown, at the start of a combat (after Priming), you may enter **Final Form** until the combat ends:
- **+2 to your primary stat** (to +10).
- **Prime two extra Surges** right away (your Primed maximum rises to match).
- **Every Surge you Unleash is Amped** (0 Flux), Signature Surges included.

## The Five Forms

### Juggernaut
**Primary Grit · Secondary Nerve · Tertiary Chutzpah · Dump Moxie and Gumption · Trees: Hide (primary), Limbs (secondary), Glands (tertiary)**

*"I don't move. You do."* The Mutagen decided you should be **unkillable** and started stacking. Juggernauts hold doorways, carry the wounded, and end arguments by standing in them. Grit and Nerve make them hard to hurt and harder to scare, and a little Chutzpah helps them loom. They're slow off the mark and not much for book-learning.

- **Play it if** you want to be the wall: Taunt, intercept, and take the hit meant for your friend.
- **Evolution nodes:** **Siege Hide** (level 4): +1 Armor, Armor max 6, reach 10 ft, counts as Huge. **Fortress** (level 7): allies near you take half Area damage, and once per round you take an ally's lethal hit.

| Stage | Name | Bio | Chrome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Juggernaut** | Thick neck, heavy brow, knuckles armoured with horn. Your skin has the give of a car tyre. | Subdermal plates you can feel through your shirt; a spine brace that whines when you stand. |
| Evolution I | **Rampart** | You grow a foot in a night. Your back splits into overlapping bone slabs; your forearms swell into shields. | Bulkhead plates bolt over shoulders and chest; your joints are hydraulic now, and they hiss. |
| Evolution II | **Colossus** | Nine feet of keratin and scar tissue. Horns, a domed skull, a heartbeat people feel through the floor. | A walking bunker: riveted armour, a face behind a visor slit, treads of steel on your soles. |
| Final | **Citadel** | Stone-hard ridges, moss in the cracks, small things nesting in you. You are a place. | Siren lights, warning stencils, a hatch in your chest. Ostrander would classify you as infrastructure. |

### Skitter
**Primary Moxie · Secondary Gumption · Tertiary Grit · Dump Nerve and Chutzpah · Trees: Wiring (primary), Limbs (secondary), Senses (tertiary)**

*"You blinked. I was already there."* The Mutagen took your nerves and set them on fire. Skitters are the fastest thing in any room: clever with their hands, wiry, and impossible to pin down. They're also jumpy, tactless and short on Flux; their Nerve is the first thing to go.

- **Play it if** you want to dart in, Daze, dart out, and act more often than anyone else.
- **Evolution nodes:** **Mantis Reflex** (level 4): two Reactions per round, and full-damage Reflex Spikes. **Lightning Step** (level 7): a half-dice weapon attack as a Quick action after you Unleash.

| Stage | Name | Bio | Chrome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Skitter** | Long limbs, twitchy hands, pupils that never settle; you're warm to the touch. | Reflex jacks at the base of the skull; fingertips with steel contacts that spark when you're nervous. |
| Evolution I | **Mantis** | Your forearms fold into barbed hooks; your knees bend backwards; you smell like ozone. | Synthetic muscle cables under translucent skin, and a capacitor hump between your shoulders. |
| Evolution II | **Wraith** | Glassy wing-cases that hum, faceted eyes, lightning crawling along your veins. | You blur when you move; a plasma coil glows through your ribs; you leave scorch-prints. |
| Final | **Livewire** | Barely solid: a figure of arcs and chitin that's always slightly somewhere else. | A human-shaped storm in a chrome cage, held together by a magnetic field. |

### Oracle
**Primary Nerve · Secondary Chutzpah · Tertiary Moxie · Dump Grit and Gumption · Trees: Senses (primary), Mind (secondary), Wiring (tertiary)**

*"I saw you coming. Three days ago."* The Mutagen gave you **too much input**, and you learned to stay calm inside it. Oracles see everything, steady everyone and hold the most Flux of any Form (their high Nerve raises their Flux Limit). They're light on their feet but physically fragile and impractical; their aim isn't.

- **Play it if** you want to shoot from a rooftop, spot every weakness, and decide which way the dice fall.
- **Evolution nodes:** **Foresight** (level 4): each round, an Omen d20 that can replace any nearby d20. **Thousand Eyes** (level 7): Target Lock on three creatures, no surprise or Blindness nearby, wider Crits.

| Stage | Name | Bio | Chrome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Oracle** | Pale, still, unsettling eyes with too much iris; you hear heartbeats through walls. | A monocle lens grafted to the socket; tiny antennae behind the ears. |
| Evolution I | **Argus** | A third eye opens on your brow; small eyes bead along your collarbones and blink out of time. | A ring of lenses around your head like a crown; your voice comes with a faint radio hiss. |
| Evolution II | **Seraph** | Wing-like membranes covered in eyes unfold from your back; you float a hand's width off the floor. | Six optic arms fan out from your spine; holographic targeting halos hang around you. |
| Final | **Panopticon** | A tall white shape of lids and lashes; when you look at someone, they feel *seen*. | A tower of lenses and dishes; every camera in the district turns toward you. |

### Alembic
**Primary Gumption · Secondary Grit · Tertiary Nerve · Dump Moxie and Chutzpah · Trees: Glands (primary), Hide (secondary), Mind (tertiary)**

*"Hold still. This will either cure you or kill it."* The Mutagen made you a **laboratory**. Alembics brew medicine and poison in their own bodies, stay standing in toxic air, and keep the whole crew alive. They're slow, tough and steady-handed, with a bedside manner best described as *blunt*.

- **Play it if** you want to heal, poison, and out-think the problem.
- **Evolution nodes:** **Transmute** (level 4): your Toxin and Corrode riders apply both Poisoned and Exposed, and your healing restores more. **Panacea** (level 7): once per combat, heal everyone nearby and end their conditions.

| Stage | Name | Bio | Chrome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Alembic** | Glands swelling at the throat and wrists; you sweat something sweet-smelling and faintly green. | Injector ports along your forearms, and a hip-mounted reservoir with a gauge. |
| Evolution I | **Still** | Translucent sacs bloom across your back, glowing with what you're brewing; your breath fogs. | Coiled tubing and glass vials under a ribcage of chrome; you gurgle when you walk. |
| Evolution II | **Bloomheart** | Spore-fronds and flower-like vents open along your spine; insects follow you. | A walking refinery: pressurised tanks, nozzle arrays, a warning placard that reads FLAMMABLE. |
| Final | **Wellspring** | A living garden: roots in the floor where you stand, fruit that heals, pollen that kills. | A mobile clinic: hissing pipes, a dozen spigots, steam that smells like hospitals. |

### Siren
**Primary Chutzpah · Secondary Moxie · Tertiary Gumption · Dump Grit and Nerve · Trees: Mind (primary), Wiring (secondary), Limbs (tertiary)**

*"Everybody look at me."* The Mutagen made you **impossible to ignore**. Sirens command attention: they talk past guards, Taunt whole rooms, and bend minds. They're quick enough to dodge the attention they draw and sharp enough to improvise, but fragile, reckless, and the first to Overload (their low Nerve means a low Flux Limit).

- **Play it if** you want to lead, talk, Taunt and control the battlefield.
- **Evolution nodes:** **Siren Song** (level 4): a Quick mass Taunt that sends every Taunted enemy at you or an ally you name (usually your Juggernaut), at −2 TN. **Crown of Will** (level 7): once per combat, a non-Boss fights for you.

| Stage | Name | Bio | Chrome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Siren** | Striking face, a voice with a second note under it, a scent people remember. | Voice modulator at the throat; LED tattoos that pulse when you talk. |
| Evolution I | **Diva** | Iridescent skin, a frill of feathers or fins at the neck; your voice harmonises with itself. | Speaker grilles bloom along your collarbones; your face projects a light-show halo. |
| Evolution II | **Choir** | A crown of soft horns; a dozen mouths open along your arms and sing in chorus. | An antenna crown and a cape of fibre-optic cable; you broadcast on every frequency. |
| Final | **Crowned** | Too beautiful to look at directly; people weep, kneel or flee. | A living billboard and a broadcast tower; your face is on every screen, saying *your* name. |

# 2. Character Creation

![A Bio freelancer](../art/char_bio.jpg)

You don't pick a race or a class. You pick **what the Mutagen is turning you into** (your Base Form), **where you came from** (Origin), and **what you've done to your body** (Augments). It takes about 30 minutes; the [web builder](https://xenodorian.github.io/BeelzRPG/builder/) does the maths for you.

## Step by Step
1. **Concept.** One sentence. *"Ex-cop with a shotgun arm who can't stop apologising."*
2. **Base Form.** Pick one of the five [Base Forms](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md): **Juggernaut, Skitter, Oracle, Alembic or Siren**. It sets your best stats, your three trees and how you'll Evolve.
3. **Stats.** Your Form gives **+3** (primary), **+2** (secondary) and **+1** (tertiary). Put **+0** and **−1** in the other two stats, in either order.
4. **Origin.** Pick or roll one (below). Write down its Knack and Perk.
5. **Knacks.** Write **two more** Knacks (3 total). Keep them narrow.
6. **Augments.** Take **6 nodes** from your Form's trees ([chapter 3](03_Augment_Trees.md)): the **Root + 2 Tier I nodes** of your primary tree, the **Root + 1 Tier I node** of your secondary tree, and the **Root** of your tertiary tree. For each tree, choose its **Material: Bio or Chrome**.
7. **Surges.** Build **3 Surges** ([Chapter 4](04_Surges.md)) and number them 1–3 on your Sequence. Slot 4 is Wild (Lab Escapees build a 4th instead).
8. **Gear.** Take one **Kit** from [Gear](08_Gear.md#starting-kits) and **50 Scrip**.
9. **Fill in the numbers** (below).
10. **Drive.** Answer three questions: *Why did you take the Mutagen? What do you want before the campaign ends? Who in Halcyon do you owe, or hate?*

## Derived Numbers (Level 1)

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | 1 (half your level, rounded up) |
| **Max HP** | 12 + (2 × Grit) + 5 per level after 1st. Recalculate when Grit changes (it does at each Evolution). |
| **Armor** | From gear and the Hide tree (max 4, unless a node says otherwise) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **Flux Limit** | 4 + Rank + Nerve (minimum 4; Splice −1) |
| **Sequence die** | d4 |
| **Prime** | 2 |
| **Signature Surges** | None yet (the first comes at Evolution I, level 4) |
| **Juice** | 1 (max 3) |
| **Defenses** | Dodge = Moxie + Rank, Endure = Grit + Rank, Resist = Nerve + Rank |

## The Five Stats
Stats are attitudes, not body parts. A gangly kid can have huge Grit; it means they won't stay down. Your Base Form decides which attitudes the Mutagen feeds, and every Evolution feeds them more (up to **+8**).

- **Grit** — *"I'm still standing."* Raw force and stubborn endurance. Use it to smash, lift, grapple, hit hard in melee, and **Endure** poison, blasts, and crushing blows. It raises your HP.
- **Moxie** — *"Watch this."* Nimble daring. Use it to sprint, climb, sneak, tumble, fight with finesse weapons, and **Dodge** attacks you can see coming. It sets initiative.
- **Nerve** — *"Breathe. Aim."* Composure and willpower. Use it to shoot, notice, track, keep calm, and **Resist** fear, mind control, and psychic attack. It holds your Mutagen together (your **Flux Limit**).
- **Gumption** — *"Hand me that wrench."* Resourceful know-how. Use it to fix, hack, patch wounds, recall lore, read a situation, and **Analyze** enemies.
- **Chutzpah** — *"You and what army?"* Shameless audacity. Use it to persuade, lie, threaten, perform, lead, **Taunt** enemies, **Rally** allies, and power psionic **Dread** Surges.

> **Which defense?** Attacks you can sidestep (claws, bullets, spit) are **Dodge**. Things you must take on the chin (gas, explosions you can't escape, disease, grabs) are **Endure**. Things that attack your mind (fear, hacking your wetware, psychic screams) are **Resist**. Every threat's stat block names the defense.

## Origins (roll d6 or choose)

| d6 | Origin | Knack | Perk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Spill-Born** — raised in the flooded Wards. | *Scavenging* | Advantage on Endure against poison, disease, and anything that adds Flux to you. |
| 2 | **Corporate Washout** — ex-Ostrander staff, fired or fled. | *Corporate Protocol* | Start with +100 Scrip. Once per mission, name an old contact who can open one door. |
| 3 | **Lab Escapee** — you were the experiment. | *Lab Science* | You know **one extra Surge** at every level (it fills a Wild slot; no effect once your Sequence has no Wild slots). It never adds a Signature Surge. |
| 4 | **Clinic Rat** — a street surgeon raised you, or still owns you. | *Street Medicine* | Your Patch action heals +Rank extra HP, and removing Scars or Faults costs you half (the doc wants favours instead). |
| 5 | **Ward Militia** — you held the barricades. | *Squad Tactics* | +2 to your roll in group initiative checks. Once per combat, take Brace as a Quick action. |
| 6 | **Bloom Convert** — the Children took you in. | *Bloom Lore* | Once per Lockdown, reroll an Overload result and choose either result. |

## Knack Examples
*Parkour, Knife Fighting, Ostrander Security, Drone Piloting, Sewer Routes, Fast Talk, Interrogation, Chemistry, Mutant Biology, Salvage Appraisal, Street Medicine, Black-Market Contacts, Crowd Work, Lockpicking, Swimming, Gambling, Old Halcyon History, Cooking Anything, Bloom Scripture, Explosives.*

A Knack should be something you could teach in a weekend course or learn on the street in a year. *"Everything Physical"* is not a Knack.

## Example: Vex (Level 1)
- **Concept:** Wiry Spill-Born courier with mantis claws who runs messages through gang turf.
- **Form:** **Skitter.** Moxie +3, Gumption +2, Grit +1; she puts **Nerve +0** and **Chutzpah −1**.
- **Origin:** Spill-Born (*Scavenging*). Knacks: *Parkour*, *Gang Turf*.
- **Augments (6):** Wiring (Bio): **Quickened** (Root), **Capacitor**, **Slipstream**. Limbs (Bio): **Natural Weapons** (Root), **Spring Legs**. Senses (Bio): **Keen** (Root). She is all Bio, so Knit heals her 1 × 3 = 3 HP after each fight.
- **Surge parts:** base (Strike, Bolt, Infuse, Kinetic) + Wiring (Shock, Blaze, Haste) + Limbs (Wave, Snare) + Senses (Glare, Veil).
- **Surges:** 1 *Mantis Lunge* (Strike + Kinetic, Moxie), 2 *Live Wire* (Bolt + Shock, Moxie via Wiring), 3 *Slip Veil* (Infuse + Veil). 4 = Wild.
- **Gear:** Sneak kit (two knives, suppressed pistol, Breach kit), Light armor.
- **Numbers:** HP 14, Armor 1, Speed 50 ft (Quickened + Spring Legs), Limit 5 (4 + 1 + 0), Dodge +4, Endure +2, Resist +1. Claws: +4 to hit, 1d8+3, +1d8 Shock once per turn (Capacitor).
- **Her Evolution I** (level 4, Mantis): Moxie +5, Gumption +3, Grit +2, Nerve +1, Chutzpah +0; Prime 3, d8 Sequence, **Mantis Reflex**, and a Signature Surge (she'll pick *Live Wire*).

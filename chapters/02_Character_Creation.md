# 2. Character Creation

![A Bio freelancer](../art/char_bio.jpg)

You don't pick a race or a class. You pick **how you carry yourself** (Stats), **where you came from** (Origin), and **what you've done to your body** (Augments). It takes about 20 minutes.

## Step by Step
1. **Concept.** One sentence. *"Ex-cop with a shotgun arm who can't stop apologising."*
2. **Stats.** Assign **+3, +2, +1, +0, −1** to Grit, Moxie, Nerve, Gumption, and Chutzpah, in any order.
3. **Origin.** Pick or roll one (below). Write down its Knack and Perk.
4. **Knacks.** Write **two more** Knacks (3 total). Keep them narrow.
5. **Augments.** Take **two nodes** from the [Augment Trees](03_Augment_Trees.md). Each tree starts with its **Root**, so you'll take either two Roots or one Root plus one node of that tree. For each tree, choose its **Form: Bio or Chrome**.
6. **Surges.** Build **3 Surges** ([Chapter 4](04_Surges.md)) and number them 1–3 on your Sequence. Slot 4 is Wild.
7. **Gear.** Take one **Kit** from [Gear](08_Gear.md#starting-kits) and **50 Scrip**.
8. **Fill in the numbers** (below).
9. **Drive.** Answer three questions: *Why did you take the Mutagen? What do you want before the campaign ends? Who in Halcyon do you owe, or hate?*

## Derived Numbers (Level 1)

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | 1 |
| **Max HP** | 12 + (2 × Grit) + 5 per level after 1st. Recalculate if Grit changes. |
| **Armor** | From gear and the Hide tree (max 4) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **Flux Limit** | 4 + Rank + Nerve (minimum 4) |
| **Sequence die** | d4 |
| **Prime** | 2 |
| **Juice** | 1 (max 3) |
| **Defenses** | Dodge = Moxie + Rank, Endure = Grit + Rank, Resist = Nerve + Rank |

## The Five Stats
Stats are attitudes, not body parts. A gangly kid can have huge Grit; it means they won't stay down.

- **Grit** — *"I'm still standing."* Raw force and stubborn endurance. Use it to smash, lift, grapple, hit hard in melee, and **Endure** poison, blasts, and crushing blows. It raises your HP.
- **Moxie** — *"Watch this."* Nimble daring. Use it to sprint, climb, sneak, tumble, fight with finesse weapons, and **Dodge** attacks you can see coming. It sets initiative.
- **Nerve** — *"Breathe. Aim."* Composure and willpower. Use it to shoot, notice, track, keep calm, and **Resist** fear, mind control, and psychic attack. It holds your Mutagen together (your **Flux Limit**).
- **Gumption** — *"Hand me that wrench."* Resourceful know-how. Use it to fix, hack, patch wounds, recall lore, read a situation, and **Analyze** enemies.
- **Chutzpah** — *"You and what army?"* Shameless audacity. Use it to persuade, lie, threaten, perform, lead, **Taunt** enemies, **Rally** allies, and power psionic **Dread** Surges.

> **Which defense?** Attacks you can sidestep (claws, bullets, spit) are **Dodge**. Things you must take on the chin (gas, explosions you can't escape, disease, grabs) are **Endure**. Things that attack your mind (fear, hacking your wetware, psychic screams) are **Resist**. Every threat's stat block names the defense.

## Origins (roll d6 or choose)

| d6 | Origin | Knack | Perk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Spill-Born** — raised in the flooded Wards. | *Scavenging* | Advantage on Endure against poison, disease, and anything that adds Flux to you. |
| 2 | **Corporate Washout** — ex-Ostrander staff, fired or fled. | *Corporate Protocol* | Start with +100 Scrip. Once per mission, name an old contact who can open one door. |
| 3 | **Lab Escapee** — you were the experiment. | *Lab Science* | You know **one extra Surge** at every level (it fills a Wild slot; no effect once your Sequence has no Wild slots). |
| 4 | **Clinic Rat** — a street surgeon raised you, or still owns you. | *Street Medicine* | Your Patch action heals +Rank extra HP, and removing Scars or Faults costs you half (the doc wants favours instead). |
| 5 | **Ward Militia** — you held the barricades. | *Squad Tactics* | +2 to your roll in group initiative checks. Once per combat, take Brace as a Quick action. |
| 6 | **Bloom Convert** — the Children took you in. | *Bloom Lore* | Once per Lockdown, reroll an Overload result and choose either result. |

## Knack Examples
*Parkour, Knife Fighting, Ostrander Security, Drone Piloting, Sewer Routes, Fast Talk, Interrogation, Chemistry, Mutant Biology, Salvage Appraisal, Street Medicine, Black-Market Contacts, Crowd Work, Lockpicking, Swimming, Gambling, Old Halcyon History, Cooking Anything, Bloom Scripture, Explosives.*

A Knack should be something you could teach in a weekend course or learn on the street in a year. *"Everything Physical"* is not a Knack.

## Example: Vex (Level 1)
- **Concept:** Wiry Spill-Born courier with mantis claws who runs messages through gang turf.
- **Stats:** Moxie +3, Grit +2, Nerve +1, Chutzpah +0, Gumption −1.
- **Origin:** Spill-Born (*Scavenging*). Knacks: *Parkour*, *Gang Turf*.
- **Augments:** Limbs Root (*Natural Weapons*, Bio) + Hide Root (*Plating*, Bio).
- **Surges:** 1 *Mantis Lunge* (Strike + Kinetic), 2 *Pin* (Strike + Snare), 3 *Carapace Flex* (Infuse + Shell). 4 = Wild.
- **Numbers:** HP 16, Armor 2 (Light armor 1 + Plating 1), Limit 6 (4 + 1 + 1), Dodge +4, Endure +3, Resist +2. Claws: +4 to hit, 1d8+3.

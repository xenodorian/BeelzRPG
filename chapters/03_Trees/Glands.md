# Glands — the walking pharmacy

*Bio: venom sacs, acid glands, healing bile, spore pods. Chrome: chem injectors, nanite reservoirs, drug pumps, nozzle ports.*

**Stat:** Gumption · **Role:** chemist, healer, attrition · **Unlocks:** Burst (Shape), Toxin, Corrode, Mend · **Primary tree of:** Alembic · **Tertiary of:** Juggernaut

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Spit.** A ranged natural attack (**d8**, 30 ft, Nerve or Gumption) dealing Toxin or Corrode damage (choose each time). Advantage on Endure against poison.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Regenerate.** At the start of your turn, if you're at half HP or less (and not Down), regain **Rank + 1** HP.
- **Stim Gland.** *Action* (or *Quick* with **Push**): you or a creature you touch regains **Rank d6 + Gumption** HP and ends Burning or Poisoned. Twice per Breather.
- **Adrenal Dump.** The first time each combat you drop to half HP or less, gain **2 × Rank + Grit** temporary HP and advantage on your next attack.
- **Antibodies.** You can't be Poisoned. *Quick,* once per Breather: end one condition on a creature you touch.

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Purge.** Once per combat, *Quick:* lose Flux equal to **Rank + 1** and take that much damage (ignores Armor).
- **Catalyst.** Your Burst Surges roll **d8** damage dice instead of d6.
- **Spore Cloud.** *Action,* once per combat, **Push:** exhale a 15-ft-radius cloud centred on you that lasts until the end of next round. Creatures outside it can't see into it. You and your allies inside are Veiled against attacks made from outside the cloud.
- **Mutagen Tap.** Once per round, when you would gain Flux, you may take **Rank + 1** damage per point instead (ignores Armor; it can't drop you below 1 HP).

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Living Pharmacy.** Stim Gland affects you and every ally within 15 ft at once. Your Mend Surges cost no Flux.

## Evolution nodes (Alembic only; granted automatically)
- **Transmute** *(level 4, Evolution I).* Your glands learn to brew anything. When your Toxin or Corrode damage applies its rider, the target is both **Poisoned and Exposed**. Your healing (Stim Gland, Mend Surges, Patch) restores **+Gumption** HP.
- **Panacea** *(level 7, Evolution II).* Your blood is medicine. Once per combat, *Action:* you and every ally within 30 ft regain **(Rank + 1)d8 + Gumption** HP, end all conditions, and lose 2 Flux. A Down ally stands up.

# Hide — the body as a fortress

*Bio: chitin plates, scales, rubbery blubber, bark-skin. Chrome: dermal plating, kinetic mesh, ablative shell.*

**Stat:** Grit · **Role:** tank, protector · **Unlocks:** Pulse (Shape), Quake, Shell · **Primary tree of:** Juggernaut · **Secondary of:** Alembic

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Plating.** +1 Armor (**+2 from Rank 3**). Armor from all sources maxes at 4, unless a node says otherwise.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Thick.** +2 Max HP per level (count past levels too).
- **Barbs.** When an enemy's melee attack damages you, it takes **2 × Rank** damage.
- **Anchor.** You can't be knocked Prone, pushed or pulled against your will. You roll defense with advantage against attacks whose rider is Held.
- **Shoulder Check.** Once per turn, you can Grapple or Shove as a Quick action.

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Adaptive.** When you take Blaze, Shock, Toxin, Corrode or Psychic damage, you resist that type (half damage) until combat ends. One type at a time; a new type replaces the old.
- **Bulwark.** *Reaction:* when an ally within 10 ft is targeted by a Single attack, you become the target instead and roll your own defense. **Push:** roll it with advantage.
- **Living Wall.** Allies adjacent to you get **+1** to defense rolls (**+2 from Rank 4**). It doesn't stack with another Living Wall.
- **Menace.** You can Taunt as a Quick action, using Grit in place of Chutzpah. You get **+2** to defense rolls against creatures you have Taunted.

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Unbreakable.** Once per round, when you fail a defense roll, **Push** to take half damage and ignore its rider. Your Armor maximum is 5.

## Evolution nodes (Juggernaut only; granted automatically)
- **Siege Hide** *(level 4, Evolution I).* Your hide thickens into overlapping slabs. +1 Armor, and your Armor maximum is **6**. Your melee reach is 10 ft, and you count as **Huge** (non-Huge creatures can't knock you Prone or push you).
- **Fortress** *(level 7, Evolution II).* You are terrain now. Allies within 10 ft of you take **half damage from Area attacks** (after their own defense roll). *Reaction:* once per round, when an ally within 30 ft would drop to 0 HP, you take that damage instead (your Armor applies).

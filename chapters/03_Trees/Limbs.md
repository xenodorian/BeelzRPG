# Limbs — claws, legs, and too many arms

*Bio: claws, mantis blades, tentacles, extra arms, digitigrade legs. Chrome: piston arms, mono-blades, grapple lines, servo-arms, hydraulic legs.*

**Stat:** Grit or Moxie · **Role:** melee, grappler, mover · **Unlocks:** Wave (Shape), Snare · **Secondary tree of:** Juggernaut, Skitter · **Tertiary of:** Siren

Limbs is the **shared body tree**. It is never a Form's primary tree, so it has no Evolution nodes. Its Apex is open to the Juggernaut and the Skitter, who have it as their secondary tree.

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Natural Weapons.** Claws, blades or pistons: a melee weapon (**d8**, Grit or Moxie) that can't be disarmed and doesn't need a free hand.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Extra Arm.** A third limb. Hold one more item, and draw, stow or interact without using your Quick action. Once per turn, when you hit with a melee attack, deal **+1 damage die** (one more of that attack's dice). **Push:** also knock the target Prone (not Huge).
- **Grapnel.** Your melee reach is 10 ft. As your Move, you can pull yourself up to 30 ft to any surface you can see, or yank an unheld object to your hand.
- **Spring Legs.** +10 ft speed. Jump 20 ft long or 10 ft high as part of your move, climb at full speed, and take half falling damage.
- **Lash Tail.** You can make an Opportunity Strike when an enemy **enters** your melee reach, as well as when it leaves. A hit from an Opportunity Strike knocks a non-Huge target Prone.

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Crusher Grip.** Once per turn when you hit with a natural weapon, the target is **Held**. Huge creatures and Bosses require a **Push**.
- **Rend.** Your natural weapons' die becomes **d10** (**d12 from Rank 4**).
- **Pounce.** Once per turn, if you move at least 15 ft toward a creature and then hit it with a melee attack, it's knocked Prone (not Huge), and you can Grapple it as a Quick action.
- **Many Hands.** Help is a Quick action for you. You can hold a second creature Held while you already hold one.

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Flurry.** When you take the Attack action with natural weapons, make a second attack with half the damage dice (round up).

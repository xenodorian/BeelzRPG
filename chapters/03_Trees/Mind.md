# Mind — the voice in every head

*Bio: swollen cortex, psionic nodules, pheromone glands, a voice that echoes. Chrome: neural coprocessor, wetware deck, voice modulator, antenna crown.*

**Stat:** Chutzpah · **Role:** psionics, hacking, control, leadership · **Unlocks:** Dread, Link · **Primary tree of:** Siren · **Secondary of:** Oracle · **Tertiary of:** Alembic

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Uplink.** Speak mind-to-mind (Bio) or by encrypted radio (Chrome) with willing creatures within 300 ft. **Mind Spike:** a ranged attack (**d8**, 60 ft, Nerve or Chutzpah) dealing Psychic damage.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Intrude.** *Action:* Gumption or Chutzpah + Rank vs a creature's TN (or a device's DC). Read its surface thoughts, or seize an unattended device for 1 minute. In combat, a Mook machine obeys you until the end of next round; any other machine is Dazed.
- **Iron Will.** Advantage on Resist, and you can't be Shaken. Allies within 30 ft get +1 to Resist.
- **Commanding Voice.** You can **Rally** as a Quick action.
- **Read the Room.** Advantage on checks to spot a lie. Once per scene, ask the GM what a creature you can see wants or fears most right now; the answer is true.

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Puppet.** *Action,* **Push:** Chutzpah + Rank vs the TN of a non-Boss creature. On a success, it immediately does one of these, your choice: moves up to its speed where you choose, or makes one attack against a target you choose. An enemy target simply takes the attack's Damage (no roll, no rider); a player target defends normally.
- **Precog.** *Reaction,* **Push:** when you or an ally within 30 ft is about to make a d20 roll (declare before rolling), they roll it with advantage.
- **Psychic Scream.** *Action,* once per combat: make one Chutzpah attack (an **Area** attack) against every enemy within 30 ft that you choose. Hit: **(Rank + 1)d6** Psychic and Shaken. Miss: half damage.
- **Glamour.** *Quick,* **Push:** you are Veiled until the end of next round.

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Hive Mind.** Once per round, an ally within 300 ft can use your Nerve or Chutzpah in place of theirs for one roll. At the start of each combat, choose one ally: they Prime one extra Surge.

## Evolution nodes (Siren only; granted automatically)
- **Siren Song** *(level 4, Evolution I).* Your voice splits into harmonies. Once per round you can Taunt as a Quick action, and your Taunt can target every enemy within 30 ft you choose (compare one roll to each creature's TN). Each enemy you Taunt this way must target **you or one ally you name** within 30 ft of you (same ally for all of them) if it can, and has **−2 TN** against that creature's defense rolls until the Taunt ends.
- **Crown of Will** *(level 7, Evolution II).* Once per combat, *Action,* **Push:** Chutzpah + Rank vs the TN of a creature within 60 ft. A **non-Boss** obeys you until the end of combat. In each Enemy Phase it acts as you choose (it won't harm itself), and it breaks free if you or an ally damages it. A **Boss** instead loses its next Interrupt and is Dazed until the end of its next turn.

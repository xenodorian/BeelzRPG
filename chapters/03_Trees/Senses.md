# Senses — eyes everywhere

*Bio: compound eyes, pit organs, bat ears, whiskers, a third eye. Chrome: optic suites, targeting HUD, radar, audio filters.*

**Stat:** Nerve · **Role:** sniper, scout, spotter · **Unlocks:** Glare, Veil · **Primary tree of:** Oracle · **Tertiary of:** Skitter

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Keen.** See in darkness out to 60 ft. Advantage on checks to notice hidden things. Bright light can't Blind you.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Target Lock.** *Quick:* mark a creature you can see. Once per turn, your attack against it deals **+1 damage die** (one more of that attack's dice). The mark lasts until combat ends or you mark another.
- **Threat Sense.** You and your group can't be surprised. *Reaction:* when an ally within 30 ft makes a defense roll, add your Nerve to it (declare before rolling).
- **Analyzer.** You can **Analyze** as a Quick action once per turn.
- **Echolocation.** You sense creatures within 30 ft without sight. You can't be Blinded against anything within that range, and hidden or invisible creatures there are revealed to you.

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Long Sight.** Double the range of your ranged attacks and ranged Surges. Ignore partial cover. Read lips at 300 ft.
- **Weak Spot.** When you Analyze a creature, its Armor and Resistances don't apply to anyone's damage until the end of its next turn.
- **Spotter.** *Quick,* once per round: an ally within 60 ft who can hear you gets advantage on its next attack against a creature you can see.
- **Hair Trigger.** When a combat starts, after Priming and before anyone else acts, you may make one ranged weapon attack.

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Deadeye.** Your ranged attacks (weapons, Spit, Mind Spike, ranged Surges) Crit on 18–20. Once per round, **Push** to reroll a missed attack.

## Evolution nodes (Oracle only; granted automatically)
- **Foresight** *(level 4, Evolution I).* A lidless third eye opens. At the start of each round, roll a d20 and set it aside: your **Omen**. Once before the round ends, after any d20 is rolled by you or an ally within 60 ft, you may replace that roll with the Omen. An unused Omen fades at the end of the round.
- **Thousand Eyes** *(level 7, Evolution II).* Eyes open all over you. Target Lock can mark up to **three** creatures at once. You and allies within 60 ft can't be surprised or Blinded. Your ranged attacks ignore partial cover, and their Crit range is one wider than it would otherwise be: 20 → 19–20, 19–20 → 18–20, 18–20 → 17–20. This is an exception to the rule that Crit ranges don't stack.

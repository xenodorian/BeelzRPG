# Wiring — faster than you should be

*Bio: hyper-fast nerves, eel-organs, adrenal crystals. Chrome: reflex boosters, synthetic muscle, capacitor banks, plasma coils.*

**Stat:** Moxie · **Role:** speed, reflexes, shock · **Unlocks:** Shock, Blaze, Haste · **Primary tree of:** Skitter · **Secondary of:** Siren · **Tertiary of:** Oracle

[← Augment Trees](../03_Augment_Trees.md)

## Root
- **Quickened.** +10 ft speed. You can **Dash** as a Quick action. Your group has advantage on initiative checks.

## Tier I (needs the Root)
- **Reflex Spike.** *Reaction,* **Push:** when you succeed on a Dodge against a melee attack, make one weapon attack against the attacker with half the damage dice (round up).
- **Capacitor.** Once per turn when you hit with a melee attack, deal **+1 damage die** (one more of that attack's dice) as Shock. **Push:** the target is also Dazed.
- **Slipstream.** You never provoke Opportunity Strikes, can move through enemies' spaces, and take **no** damage when you succeed on a Dodge against an Area attack.
- **Wall Run.** As part of your Move you can run along walls and across liquids, as long as you end the Move on something that holds you (otherwise you fall).

## Tier II (needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in this tree)
- **Overclock.** Once per Breather, take one extra Action on your turn, then gain 1 Flux.
- **Arc Jump.** Once per turn, as part of your Move, flash up to 20 ft in a straight line, passing through creatures' spaces. **Push:** each creature you pass through takes **2 × Rank** Shock damage.
- **Kinetic Battery.** Each time you succeed on a Dodge, store a charge (max 3). When you hit with an attack, you may spend all your charges: **+1 damage die per charge**, as Shock. Charges fade when combat ends.
- **Afterimage.** When you Dash, you are Veiled until the start of your next turn.

## Apex (needs level 7+, 6 other nodes in this tree, and this must be your primary or secondary tree)
- **Blur.** You get two Moves each turn. If you've moved 20+ ft this turn, roll your first Dodge each round with advantage until your next turn.

## Evolution nodes (Skitter only; granted automatically)
- **Mantis Reflex** *(level 4, Evolution I).* Your nerves fire before you think. You get **two Reactions** per round (both refresh at the start of your turn). Your Reflex Spike and Opportunity Strikes use your full damage dice.
- **Lightning Step** *(level 7, Evolution II).* You move between moments. Once per turn, after you Unleash a Surge, you may make **one weapon attack as a Quick action**.

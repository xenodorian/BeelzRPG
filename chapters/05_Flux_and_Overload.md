# 5. Flux & Overload

![A Splice freelancer](../art/char_splice.jpg)

Every Surge, every Push, every Churn floods your cells (or circuits) with Mutagen. **Flux** measures how close you are to losing control.

## Flux
- Your Flux starts each mission at **0** (Chrome freelancers who skipped Upkeep start higher).
- You **gain** Flux when you:
  - **Unleash** a Surge: 1, +1 per Twist (0 if Amped).
  - **Churn:** 1.
  - **Push** an Augment: 1.
  - Are hit by a **Mutagenic** threat: as its stat block says.
- Your **Flux Limit** = **4 + Rank + Nerve** (minimum 4; Splice −1).
- Flux **drops to 0** when you take a Breather or a Lockdown, and when you Overload.
- Flux never stops you from acting. You can always choose to go over.

## Overload
The moment your Flux goes **above** your Limit, you **Overload**:
1. Take **Rank d6** damage (ignores Armor). This can't drop you below 1 HP.
2. Roll **d12** on your Form's table: **Bloom** if all your trees are Bio, **Glitch** if all are Chrome. Splices roll both and pick one.
3. Set your Flux to **0**.

Results last **until the end of your next turn** unless they say otherwise. **Scars** and **Faults** are permanent.

> **Why go over?** Sometimes the fight is worth it. An Overload resets your Flux and the table has a few gifts. Most results hurt, though, and doing it twice in a fight is a good way to end up Down.

## Last Gasp
Once per Lockdown, when you would drop to 0 HP, you may **Overload on purpose** instead: skip the damage step, roll on your table, and stay at **1 HP**. Your body refuses to die, and pays for it.

## Mutagenic Threats
Some enemies are **Mutagenic** (see [Threats](10_Threats.md)). When their attack damages you, you also gain the listed Flux. This is how the city slowly changes you.

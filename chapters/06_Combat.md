# 6. Combat

Combat is fast because the GM barely rolls. Players attack enemies' **Guard**; players defend against enemies' **TN**. Enemies move together, so the GM resolves them in one sweep.

## Starting a Fight
1. **Who goes first?**
   - If the freelancers start it (ambush, first shot), **players go first**.
   - If enemies ambush, the group makes a **Nerve** group check (notice) vs the enemies' highest TN. Failure: enemies take a free Enemy Phase first; after it, players go first.
   - Otherwise, the group makes a **Moxie** group check vs the enemies' highest TN. Success: players go first. Failure: enemies go first.
2. **Prime.** Every freelancer rolls their Sequence dice ([Surges](04_Surges.md#priming)).
3. Run rounds.

## The Round
Each round has two phases:
1. **Player Phase.** Players take turns in any order they agree on, one at a time. A player can **wait** and go later in the phase, as long as they haven't done anything yet this turn.
2. **Enemy Phase.** Every enemy gets a **Move and one action** (usually an attack; Elites attack twice, Bosses see [Threats](10_Threats.md)). The GM resolves the enemies one at a time, in any order; targeted players roll defense. At the start of each enemy's turn, first resolve what happens "at the start of its turn": Burning, Regenerating, and a Boss ending one condition on itself.

Then the next round starts. If enemies went first, the round is Enemy Phase then Player Phase.

**Durations.** "Until the end of its next turn" means the end of that creature's next turn. For enemies, their turn is the Enemy Phase, so a condition you put on an enemy during the Player Phase lasts through the coming Enemy Phase and ends when that phase ends. **"Until the end of next round"** means through the whole round after the current one.

**Bosses' Interrupts.** A Boss also gets an **Interrupt**: one action (no Move) right after any player's turn, once per round ([Threats](10_Threats.md#3-role)).

## Your Turn
On your turn you get a **Move**, an **Action**, and a **Quick action**, in any order. You also get **one Reaction** per round (it refreshes at the start of your turn; the Skitter's Mantis Reflex gives two).

**Move.** Up to your speed (30 ft; 6 squares on a grid). You can split it around your Action. Climbing, swimming, crawling, and difficult terrain cost double. Standing from Prone costs half your speed.

### Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Weapon or natural attack: d20 + stat + Rank vs Guard. Damage = **Rank × weapon die + stat**. |
| **Unleash** | Use a Primed Surge or a Signature Surge ([Chapter 4](04_Surges.md)). |
| **Dash** | Move again. |
| **Brace** | Advantage on all defense rolls until your next turn. |
| **Help** | An ally within 30 ft gets advantage on their next roll before your next turn. Describe how. |
| **Patch** | Use a Medkit charge on a creature you touch: Gumption check, DC 10. Success: it regains **Rank d6 + Gumption** HP (a Down creature stands up). Failure: the charge is still used, but it regains 1 HP. |
| **Analyze** | Gumption + Rank vs the TN of a creature you can see within 60 ft. Success: learn its stat block (or one weakness) and it is **Exposed** until the end of its next turn. |
| **Taunt** | Chutzpah + Rank vs the TN of a creature you can see within 60 ft. Success: in its next Enemy Phase, it must target you if it can. |
| **Rally** | Chutzpah check, DC 13. Success: every ally within 60 ft who can hear you gains **Rank + Chutzpah** temporary HP. Once per combat per ally. |
| **Grapple / Shove** | Grit + Rank vs the Guard of a creature in your melee reach. Success: the target is **Held**, or pushed 10 ft, or knocked **Prone** (not Huge). |
| **Other** | Hack a door, haul a lever, run a check. If it takes about 6 seconds, it's an Action. |

### Quick Actions
Draw or stow an item, open a door, drink a stim, speak a few sentences, **Churn**, **Target Lock** (Senses), or anything the rules call Quick.

### Reactions
You have **one Reaction** per round. Spend it on:
- **Opportunity Strike:** when an enemy leaves your melee reach, make one melee weapon attack against it.
- Reactions from Augments (Bulwark, Reflex Spike, Threat Sense, Precog, Fortress).

### Opportunity Strikes
- **Leaving reach provokes.** When a creature moves out of an enemy's melee reach, that enemy may make one melee attack against it right then (a freelancer attacks with a melee weapon; an enemy uses its melee attack and you defend).
- **One per round.** A freelancer's Opportunity Strike uses their Reaction. Each enemy can make **one Opportunity Strike per round**. A Dazed creature can't make one.
- **Moving carefully:** move at half speed (each 5 ft costs 10 ft; each square costs 2) and you provoke no Opportunity Strikes for that movement.
- Teleporting, being pushed or pulled, and Slipstream never provoke.

## Positioning: Theatre of the Mind or Grid
Mutagen plays equally well with **theatre of the mind** or on a **battle map**. Pick one per table (or per fight). Every distance in the book is in feet; this is how to read it either way.

| Distance | Theatre of the mind | Grid (1 square = 5 ft) |
|---|---|---|
| Touch, adjacent, melee reach | **Close** | 1 square, diagonals included |
| Reach 10 ft (Huge, Grapnel, Pole Haft) | Close, or one step away | 2 squares |
| 30 ft (one Move, Help, Infuse, Shotgun) | **Near** | 6 squares |
| 60 ft (Bolt, Burst, Pistol, Analyze, Taunt, Rally) | **Far** | 12 squares |
| 120 ft (Rifle, a Bolt with Reach) | Far | 24 squares |
| Beyond | **Distant** | off the map |

### Ranges (both ways)
- **Melee reach** is 5 ft (adjacent) unless a feature extends it.
- **Ranged attacks** reach their listed range. You can shoot up to **double** that range with disadvantage, except with Area weapons, grenades and Surges (those stop at their listed range).
- **A shooter next to an enemy is distracted.** Ranged attacks are at disadvantage while an enemy is adjacent to the attacker. When an *enemy* fires a ranged attack while a freelancer is adjacent to it, the target defends with advantage.
- **Line of sight:** you need to see a target to attack it at range or to Analyze, Taunt or Target Lock it. Creatures don't block sight; walls and big obstacles do.
- **Pushes** move the target directly away from you.

### Areas (both ways)
An Area hits every creature inside it; each one rolls its own defense (enemies compare your one attack to their Guard).
- **Burst** "N-ft radius" (or "N ft around it"): every creature within N ft of a point. On a grid, a square of N÷5 squares around the point: a 10-ft burst covers 5×5 squares, 20 ft covers 9×9. "Around it" leaves out the creature itself.
- **Cone** N ft: from you, N÷5 squares deep, widening one square on each side per square (a quarter-circle).
- **Line** N ft: from you, N÷5 squares long and one square wide.
- **Walls stop Areas.** A square the centre of the Area can't see is not in it.
- **Allies in your Area:** your Burst and Wave hit allies inside too (they roll Dodge against your attack total; success = half damage, no rider). Pulses, Psychic Scream and anything that says "creatures you choose" hit only who you choose.
- **Who's in it, theatre of the mind:** the GM decides from the fiction. As a guide for a party of four that isn't bunching up or spreading out, a 10-ft Area catches its target and about one in three of the others, 20 ft about half, 30 ft about two in three. Tell the players when they're bunched up.

### On a Grid
- **Distance** is the larger of the two gaps (diagonals count as one square).
- **Every creature fills one square**, Huge ones included (or 2×2 if your map has room). You can move through allies but not enemies, and you can't end your move in an occupied square.
- **Walls and big obstacles** block movement, line of sight and Areas (trace a line from centre to centre). Fliers ignore obstacles when moving.
- Summoned creatures appear in free squares next to their summoner.

## Attacking
Roll **d20 + stat + Rank** against the target's **Guard**.
- **Melee:** the stat listed for the weapon in [Gear](08_Gear.md) (Grit for heavy hitting, Moxie for finesse; many weapons allow either).
- **Ranged:** Nerve. Long range (up to double, with disadvantage) and shooting with an enemy next to you: see [Ranges](#ranges-both-ways).
- **Crit** on a natural 20: roll the damage dice twice.

## Defending
When an enemy attacks you, roll **d20 + defense stat + Rank** against its **TN**. The stat block names the defense:
- **Dodge** (Moxie): claws, bullets, spit, anything you can see coming.
- **Endure** (Grit): gas, blasts, disease, crushing grabs.
- **Resist** (Nerve): fear, psychic attacks, hacking your wetware.

**Single** attacks: success = no damage. **Area** attacks: success = half damage. Failure = full damage plus any rider.

**Enemy Opportunity Strikes.** If you leave an enemy's melee reach on your turn, it gets a free attack on you right then (you defend; one per enemy per round, [above](#opportunity-strikes)). Move carefully (half speed) to slip away safely.

## Damage
- **Armor** reduces each instance of damage by its value (minimum 1). Armor doesn't reduce Psychic damage or anything that "ignores Armor."
- **Resistance** halves damage of a type; **Vulnerability** doubles it.
- **Temporary HP** soaks damage first and doesn't stack (keep the higher). It fades at the end of combat.
- **Cover:** partial cover gives you +2 to defense rolls (and gives enemies +2 Guard). Full cover means you can't be targeted.
- **Damage types:** Kinetic, Blaze, Shock, Toxin, Corrode, Psychic.

## Conditions
All conditions last **until the end of the affected creature's next turn** unless a rule says otherwise. Reapplying a condition refreshes it; it doesn't stack. **Bosses** end one condition on themselves at the start of their turn in every Enemy Phase.

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | Can't see. Disadvantage on attacks (a Blinded enemy's targets defend with advantage); attacks against it have advantage (a Blinded player defends with disadvantage). |
| **Burning** | Takes **2 × the source's Rank** Blaze damage at the start of its next turn (for enemies: at the start of its turn in the Enemy Phase), then the flames die down. Healing that ends Burning (Mend, Stim Gland) puts it out before it bites. |
| **Dazed** | No Reaction or Quick action (so no Opportunity Strikes). A Dazed enemy can't use Specials, and a Dazed Boss gets only one action. |
| **Exposed** | −2 Guard (enemies) or −2 to defense rolls (players). |
| **Held** | Speed 0. Ends early if the holder is moved away, Dazed, or Down. |
| **Poisoned** | Deals half damage. |
| **Prone** | Melee attacks against it have advantage; ranged attacks against it have disadvantage. Standing costs half speed. |
| **Shaken** | Disadvantage on attacks and checks; can't willingly move closer to the source. A Shaken enemy's targets defend with advantage. |
| **Veiled** | Advantage on defense rolls and on its next attack. |
| **Down** | See below. |

> **Enemy advantage → player disadvantage.** Since enemies don't roll, "attacks against a Prone player have advantage" means that player defends with **disadvantage**.

## Going Down
At **0 HP** you are **Down**: fall Prone, drop what you're holding, and you can only crawl 5 ft and speak.
- At the end of each Enemy Phase, roll **Endure (DC 10)**. Failure: mark a **Death mark**. A natural 20: stand up with 1 HP.
- You can still be attacked while Down; you defend normally (Prone: disadvantage against melee). Taking damage while Down marks a Death mark (two on a Crit or failed defense vs a Boss).
- **3 Death marks: you die.**
- Any healing stands you up with that much HP. Death marks clear on a Breather.
- When you go Down, roll on the **Injury** table. Injuries last until your next Lockdown.
- **Last Gasp** ([Chapter 5](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#last-gasp)) can keep you up once per Lockdown.
- **If every freelancer is Down or dead, the fight is lost.** The enemies win the scene: the GM decides what they do with the fallen (capture, strip their gear, drag them off as Samples, or leave them for dead with their Endure rolls still running). It's a setback, not automatically the end.

### Injury Table (d6)

| d6 | Injury (until Lockdown) |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Cracked Ribs.** −5 Max HP. |
| 2 | **Concussion.** Disadvantage on Gumption checks. |
| 3 | **Torn Limb.** Disadvantage on attacks with one chosen limb (your choice: arms or legs → speed −10 ft instead). |
| 4 | **Ruptured Gland / Fried Port.** Your Flux Limit is −2. |
| 5 | **Shell Shock.** Disadvantage on Resist. |
| 6 | **Lucky Break.** Just a scar. No effect. |

## Ending a Fight
Combat ends when one side is gone, flees, or gives up. Unused Primed Surges fade, temporary HP fades, and Bio freelancers' **Knit** heals them (a Down freelancer healed this way gets up). Enemies at half HP or less with a sense of self-preservation usually try to flee or surrender; at the GM's call, a Chutzpah check can make them.

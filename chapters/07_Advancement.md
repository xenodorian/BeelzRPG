# 7. Advancement

**One mission, one level.** When the party completes a GRAFT contract (successfully or not, as long as they survive and learn something), everyone gains a level. A full campaign is **10 missions**: levels 1 to 10, with the finale played at level 10. For shorter games, start at level 3 or 5.

## Each Level
1. **+5 Max HP** (+2 more with Hide: Thick).
2. **+1 Augment node.**
3. **+1 Surge** (build it from your parts). You may also **rewrite** one existing Surge.
4. Recalculate Rank, Flux Limit, and defenses.
5. Optionally **respec** one Augment node.

**Stat boost:** +1 to two different stats (max +5).

**Final Form (level 10):** once per Lockdown, at the start of a combat, enter Final Form: every Surge you Unleash this combat is Amped, and you Prime two extra Surges.

## Samples
Bosses and some Elites carry a **Sample**: a piece of their mutated biology or chrome. After defeating one, a freelancer can harvest it with a Gumption check (DC = its TN). Between missions, a GRAFT clinic can splice it into you **for 100 Scrip**:
- It becomes a new Surge in one of your **Wild slots**. It may use the Boss's signature Shape and Effect even if you haven't unlocked them.
- If you have no Wild slots, it replaces a Surge you know.

GRAFT technically owns all Samples. Selling one on the black market pays 300 Scrip × its Threat level and is a great way to get a mission about it.

## Pay
GRAFT pays **200 + (100 × mission level) Scrip** per freelancer on completion (300 at level 1), plus bonuses the GM sets. See [Gear](08_Gear.md) for prices.

## Respec and Clinics
Installing nodes happens off-screen at a clinic between missions. Removing a Scar or Fault costs 200 Scrip. A Lab Escapee's or Clinic Rat's Origin may change who does the work and what it costs.

# 9. Running the Game

This chapter is for the GM. Everything a player needs is in chapters 1–8.

## Your Job
1. **Describe Halcyon** vividly and briefly: smell, sound, one weird detail.
2. **Ask questions** and use the answers. *"Vex, which gang tattooed you?"*
3. **Call for rolls only when failure is interesting.** Otherwise say yes.
4. **Make failures move the story:** fail, succeed at a cost, or things get worse. Never "nothing happens."
5. **Hand out Juice** generously; roughly one per player per session, more for great moments.

## Setting DCs
Pick the DC from the [Core Rules](01_Core_Rules.md#difficulty-dc) table: **13 when unsure.** Add advantage or disadvantage for circumstances instead of fiddling with numbers. If an enemy is opposing the check directly, use its **TN**.

## Running the Enemy Phase
- Declare all enemy actions at once: *"The two Cleaners fire at Vex and Juno; the Drain Beast grabs Ozzie."*
- Players roll their defenses; you just read out Damage and riders.
- Mooks and Soldiers act simply. Elites and Bosses should feel smart: focus the weak, punish clumping, flee when beaten.
- **Spread attacks** unless the fiction says otherwise. Focusing one player every round is how you get a death in a Standard fight.
- Let the fiction override the grid: a Brute can smash a wall, a Skirmisher can jump down a stairwell.

## Missions
GRAFT contracts follow a simple shape. Each is about one session.
1. **Brief.** A GRAFT handler explains the job and the pay. Players ask questions, buy gear.
2. **Approach.** Getting in: travel, a social obstacle, or a skill challenge.
3. **Trouble.** One or two fights or hazards. Something is not what the brief said.
4. **Heart.** The objective: a boss, a choice, a sample, a person.
5. **Out.** Escape, consequences, a Breather if earned.
6. **Debrief.** Get paid, level up, downtime.

### The Spill Clock
Give each mission a **6-segment clock**. Tick it when players dawdle, make noise, Overload, or fail a roll badly. When it fills, the situation escalates: reinforcements, a flood, a Cleaner squad, the building collapses. Then reset it. Show it to the players; it makes them hurry.

### Skill Challenges
For a chase, a hack, or a climb, ask for **3 successes before 3 failures** (Standard DC). Each player says how they help and picks a stat. Each failure costs something: HP (T-level Damage), a tick on the Spill Clock, or lost gear.

## Downtime (between missions)
Each freelancer picks **one**:
- **Clinic:** install your new node, splice a Sample, remove a Scar or Fault.
- **Side Gig:** earn 50 × level Scrip.
- **Dig:** learn a secret about the next mission (the GM answers one question truthfully).
- **Rest:** clear all Injuries and gain 1 Juice at the start of next session.
- **Bond:** spend time with a contact; they'll do you a favour on the next mission.

## Rewards
- **Levels:** one per mission ([Advancement](07_Advancement.md)).
- **Scrip:** 200 + (100 × mission level) each, plus bonuses for style or recovered Samples.
- **Samples:** from Bosses and some Elites.
- **Juice:** in the moment.

## Pacing Guidelines
- **Fights:** 2–3 per mission. One should be Hard. The boss can be Deadly if the players found a way to soften it.
- **Breathers:** let the party take them, but make the clock tick.
- **Surges:** encourage them. If a player never Unleashes, ask what their body is doing. Surges are the fun.
- **Overload:** describe it gleefully, never as a punishment. It's the setting coming through the character.

## Safety
Body horror can hit hard. Before play, agree as a table what's off-limits (lines) and what should happen off-screen (veils). Anyone can pause a scene, no questions asked.

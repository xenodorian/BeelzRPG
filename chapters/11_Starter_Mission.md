# 11. Starter Mission — *Pumpstation 9*

A one-session mission for **3–5 level 1 freelancers**. Five pregenerated characters (one per Base Form) are at the end. Numbers assume 4 players; each fight lists what to change for 3 or 5 (checked in `tools/sim_results/11_starter_mission.md`).

## The Brief
*Handler Dez Okafor, GRAFT Field Office 3, over a crackling radio:*
> "Pumpstation 9 keeps the Fenwick Ward from flooding. Since Tuesday it's been pumping **Mutagen** into their water instead of out. Ward council's paying us; we're paying you. **300 Scrip each** to shut the intake and kill whatever's in there. Bring back anything weird. Don't drink anything."

**What's really going on:** A Scrapjack crew, paid by an Ostrander Remnant agent, planted a Brood Matriarch egg in the intake to test whether a mutagenic bloom can be weaponized through a water supply. The Matriarch has rooted into the pipes. The Scrapjacks are still here, stripping the pump room and waiting for their pickup.

**Spill Clock:** 6 segments. Tick it for loud noises, long rests, failed stealth, and Overloads. When it fills, see *Out*.

## Scene 1: The Fenwick Toll
The only dry road to the pumpstation crosses a Ward barricade. **Three Ward Toughs** (T1 Soldiers, MR 1) want a 50-Scrip "toll" each.
- **Talk:** Chutzpah DC 13 lets the party through free; with a good argument (*"we're here to fix your water"*) roll with advantage.
- **Fight (Standard, 3 units):** 3 Ward Toughs (MR 1 → 1 unit each). For 3 players use 2; for 5 use 4. They're Cowardly (they surrender at half HP), and the Ward council will dock the party's pay by 100.
- **Sneak:** A group Moxie check DC 13 through the flooded alleys. Failure ticks the clock and they arrive soaked (each takes 2 damage from the cold).
- **Clue:** Any Tough knows "some chrome-heads went in Monday with a crate."

## Scene 2: The Pump Hall
A three-storey hall of rusting turbines, waist-deep green water on the lower floor, catwalks above. **A Scrapjack Picker** is prying copper from the turbines while **two Spill Rats** nest in the pipes. More Pickers are asleep upstairs (a second one joins if the Spill Clock ticks during the fight).
- **Fight (Standard, 3 units):** 1 Picker (MR 2 → 1½) + 2 Spill Rats (MR 1 → 1 each). For 3 players: 1 Picker + 1 Rat. For 5: the same fight (Packs focus one target, so a bigger party doesn't make Downs much rarer: 26% with 5 players vs 27% with 4); add a third Rat only for a Hard fight. The Rats attack anyone in the water first, all on the same freelancer (Pack, Overwhelm). *v0.2's 2 Pickers + 4 Rats is a Deadly 7 units under the new table: an 82% chance someone goes Down.*
- **Terrain:** Water is difficult terrain. Catwalks give partial cover. A turbine can be restarted (Gumption DC 13, Action): everyone in the water takes 7 Shock (**Area**, Endure for half).
- **Twist:** A Picker carries an Ostrander-coded **data chit** and a radio: their pickup is "in one hour." Reading the chit (Gumption DC 16 or a Hack deck) reveals the plan.
- If a Picker is captured: Chutzpah DC 13 to make them talk. They know the egg went "into the big intake pipe."

**Breather:** The control room is defensible. The party can take a Breather here (tick the clock twice).

## Scene 3: The Intake
A flooded concrete cathedral where the river pipe meets the pumps. Glowing roots of flesh cover the walls. In the middle, fused to the intake grate, is the **Brood Matriarch** with **two Spill Rats** already crawling on her.
- **Fight (Hard):** the Matriarch alone (BR 1, a whole Hard budget); she Births her own Brood Spawn. **Scale her for the party** ([Threats §6](10_Threats.md#6-party-size)): HP and all Damage ×¾ for 3 players (HP 43), ×1¼ for 5 (HP 72). The two Spill Rats on her flee into the pipes when the fight starts. See [Threats](10_Threats.md#the-brood-matriarch).
- **The Valve:** A huge manual valve stands 20 ft from the Matriarch. **Three successful Grit or Gumption checks (DC 13, Action each)** close it. When it closes, she shrieks, is **Dazed** until the end of the next Enemy Phase, and can't Birth for the rest of the fight. Closing the valve also completes the mission objective, even if she survives.
- **The Sample:** Harvest with Gumption DC 12 after she dies: *Brood Web* (Burst + Snare).

## Out
- If the clock filled at any point, **two Ostrander Cleaners** (T3 Soldiers, MR 3 → 2 units each: Standard-to-Hard at level 1 by the sims, Deadly for a tired party) arrive by boat as the party leaves. Running is the smart choice: a group Moxie check DC 13 to reach the alleys. On failure, the Cleaners get one Enemy Phase of shots before the party escapes.
- If the clock never filled, the pickup boat arrives empty; the party can ambush it and capture the Ostrander agent, **Mr. Hale**, a calm man with no mutations and an expensive umbrella.

## Debrief
- Pay: 300 Scrip each; +100 each for the data chit or Mr. Hale; −100 total if they fought the Ward.
- GRAFT asks for the Sample. Players who hand it over get +50 each. Players who keep it can splice it.
- Everyone reaches **level 2.**
- **Hook:** the data chit lists five more pumpstations.

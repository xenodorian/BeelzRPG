# 12. Quick Reference

## The Roll
**d20 + Stat (+ Rank)** ≥ target. Rank always on attacks and defenses; on checks only with a Knack. Ties: player wins.  
**Rank** = level ÷ 2, round up (1–5). **Adv/Dis:** 2d20 keep high/low. Nat 20 attack = Crit (double dice). Nat 20 defense = no damage + attacker Exposed. Nat 1 = fail.

**Nodes:** L1 = 3 primary + 2 secondary + 1 tertiary. Each level: +1 primary (auto) + 1 secondary *or* tertiary. Tier II: L4+ & 3 nodes in tree. Apex: L7+ & 6 other nodes, primary/secondary only. Primary full at L8 → overflow.

## Stats
**Grit** smash/endure/HP · **Moxie** dodge/sneak/finesse · **Nerve** aim/notice/resist/Limit · **Gumption** fix/hack/heal/know · **Chutzpah** talk/lie/taunt/rally/Dread  
**Defenses:** Dodge = Moxie+Rank · Endure = Grit+Rank · Resist = Nerve+Rank

## Derived
HP 12 + 2×Grit, +5/level · Flux Limit 4 + Rank + Nerve (Splice −1) · Speed 30 · Armor max 4 (Unbreakable 5, Siege Hide 6) · Juice start 1, max 3

## Round
**Player Phase** (any order) → **Enemy Phase** (all at once, players defend).  
**Turn:** Move + Action + Quick; 1 Reaction per round.  
**Actions:** Attack · Unleash · Dash · Brace · Help · Patch · Analyze · Taunt · Rally · Grapple/Shove · Other  
**Quick:** draw, stim, Churn, Target Lock, talk  
**Damage:** Rank × weapon die + stat. Armor −X per hit (min 1). Area: miss/successful defense = half.

## Surges
**Prime** at combat start: roll Sequence die × Prime (2; 3 from L4; 4 from L7). Doubles = **Amped** (0 Flux). Wild slot = roll Wild table (each separately, never doubles).  
**Unleash** (Action): pay 1 Flux + 1 per Twist; a missed Strike/Bolt stays Primed. **Churn** (Quick): +1 Flux, roll once more; not on a turn you Unleash (max Prime + 1 held).  
**Signature Surges** (1 from L4, 2 from L7): always Primed, once per round each, normal Flux, not on the Sequence.  
**Surge stat:** the Shape's stat or the stat of the Effect's tree (Hide Grit · Limbs Grit/Moxie · Senses Nerve · Glands Gumption · Wiring Moxie · Mind Chutzpah).  
**Juice:** reroll a d20 · choose a Prime · end a condition.

## Flux & Overload
Gain Flux: Unleash, Churn, Push. Over Limit → **Overload:** take Rank d6 (min 1 HP left), roll d12 Bloom (Bio) / Glitch (Chrome) / both, pick (Splice), Flux → 0. Breather/Lockdown → Flux 0. **Last Gasp:** once per Lockdown, at 0 HP Overload instead and stay at 1 HP.

## Conditions (until end of affected creature's next turn)
**Blinded** dis. on attacks, attackers adv. · **Burning** 2×source Rank dmg at turn start; spend Move to end · **Dazed** no Quick/Reaction/Specials; Boss 1 action · **Exposed** −2 Guard / −2 defense · **Held** speed 0 · **Poisoned** half damage dealt · **Prone** melee adv./ranged dis. against; stand = ½ speed · **Shaken** dis. on attacks & checks, can't approach source · **Veiled** adv. on defense & next attack  
*Enemy advantage = player rolls defense with disadvantage.*

## Down
0 HP: Prone, crawl 5 ft. End of each Enemy Phase: Endure DC 10, fail = Death mark (nat 20: up at 1 HP). Damage while Down = mark. 3 marks = dead. Any healing = up. Roll Injury (d6).

## Rest
**Breather** (1 hr, 2 per Lockdown): regain ½ Max HP (+Knit, which also triggers after each combat), Flux 0, clear Death marks. **Lockdown** (8 hr safe): full reset.

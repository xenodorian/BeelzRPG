# 13. Character Building Guide

![Chrome, Bio, and Splice freelancers](../art/char_splice.jpg)

This chapter walks you from a blank sheet to a finished level 1 freelancer, then through both **Evolutions** to level 10. It tells you **what to pick and in what order**. The rules themselves live in chapters 1–8; when they disagree with this guide, they win.

## Level 1, Step by Step

### 1. Concept
One sentence with a **job**, a **body change**, and a **flaw**: *"Ex-Ward electrician who wired her voice into the power grid and can't stop broadcasting."*

### 2. Base Form
Pick the Form that matches what you want to **do** in a fight:

| You want to... | Form | Primary stat | Trees (primary / secondary / tertiary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hold the line, protect, hit hard up close | **Juggernaut** | Grit | Hide / Limbs / Glands |
| Be fast, slippery and deadly in melee | **Skitter** | Moxie | Wiring / Limbs / Senses |
| Shoot, spot, and bend luck | **Oracle** | Nerve | Senses / Mind / Wiring |
| Heal, poison, out-think | **Alembic** | Gumption | Glands / Hide / Mind |
| Talk, Taunt, control, lead | **Siren** | Chutzpah | Mind / Wiring / Limbs |

### 3. Stats
Your Form sets **+3 / +2 / +1**. Put **+0** and **−1** in the two stats left over. Pick the −1 for the stat you'll be tested on least. A −1 in Grit costs 2 HP and a weak Endure; a −1 in Moxie means a weak Dodge (the most common defense); a −1 in Nerve means a weak Resist and a lower Flux Limit.

### 4. Origin
Roll d6 or choose. Write its **Knack** and **Perk**. Perks with numbers: *Corporate Washout* +100 Scrip; *Lab Escapee* +1 Surge known; *Clinic Rat* +Rank to Patch; *Ward Militia* +2 on group initiative.

### 5. Knacks
Your Origin gives one; write **two more** (3 total). Narrow and concrete: *Sewer Routes*, not *Survival*. You gain one more at levels 3, 6 and 9.

### 6. Augments
Take **6 nodes**, only from your Form's trees:

| Tree | Nodes at level 1 |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Root + **2 Tier I** |
| **Secondary** | Root + **1 Tier I** |
| **Tertiary** | Root |

For **each tree**, choose its **Material** (Bio or Chrome) when you take the Root:

| You have | You are | Perk | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only Bio trees | **Bio** | Knit: +Rank HP per Bio tree after each fight and Breather | Bloom table |
| Only Chrome trees | **Chrome** | Firmware: swap one Primed Surge per fight | Glitch table, disadvantage vs Shock, **25 Scrip Upkeep per Chrome tree per mission** |
| Both | **Splice** | Knit (counting Bio trees) + Firmware (Chrome-tree parts) + roll both Overload tables, choose one | Chrome's costs above, **Flux Limit −1** |

**What each Root unlocks** (your Surge parts, fixed by your Form): Hide → Pulse, Quake, Shell · Limbs → Wave, Snare · Senses → Glare, Veil · Glands → Burst, Toxin, Corrode, Mend · Wiring → Shock, Blaze, Haste · Mind → Dread, Link.

Three Roots give you a weapon-like attack: **Natural Weapons** (Limbs, d8 melee, Grit/Moxie), **Spit** (Glands, d8, 30 ft, Nerve/Gumption) and **Mind Spike** (Mind, d8, 60 ft, Nerve/Chutzpah).

### 7. Surges and the Sequence
Build **3 Surges** (4 for a Lab Escapee) and write them in Sequence slots **1–3**. Your **Sequence die is d4**, so slot **4 is Wild** (a Lab Escapee fills it). Prime is **2**.

**The Surge recipe:**
1. **Shape:** any base Shape (**Strike, Bolt, Infuse**) or one your Roots unlocked.
2. **Effect:** a **damage Effect** (base: **Kinetic**) for Strike/Bolt/Burst/Wave/Pulse, **or** an **Infuse Effect** for Infuse. Never mix. Every Form has at least two Infuse Effects.
3. **Attack stat:** the Shape's stat **or** the stat of the tree that unlocked the Effect (Hide Grit · Limbs Grit/Moxie · Senses Nerve · Glands Gumption · Wiring Moxie · Mind Chutzpah). This is how every Form attacks with its primary stat.
4. **Twist** (optional): **one** at Rank 1–2, **two different** from Rank 3. Each must match the Shape (*Chain*: Strike/Bolt; *Linger*: area Shapes; *Leech*, *Overcharge*: damage only; *Sure*, *Reach*, *Hush*: any).
5. **Cost:** 1 Flux + 1 per Twist.
6. **Name it and describe it** in your Form's look.

**Numbers on a Surge:**

| Shape | Attack | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Strike (melee) | Grit or Moxie (or Effect's tree stat) + Rank | (Rank+1)d10 + stat |
| Bolt (60 ft) | Nerve (or Effect's tree stat) + Rank | (Rank+1)d8 + stat |
| Burst (10-ft radius, 60 ft) | Nerve (or Effect's tree stat) + Rank | (Rank+1)d6, no stat |
| Wave (15-ft cone / 30-ft line) | Grit or Nerve (or Effect's tree stat) + Rank | (Rank+1)d6, no stat |
| Pulse (10 ft around you) | Grit (or Effect's tree stat) + Rank | (Rank+1)d6, no stat |
| Infuse (you or an ally, 30 ft) | no roll | per Effect |

Add **+1 die** for Kinetic and **+2 dice** for Overcharge. Burst and Wave hit allies in the area too.

**A good first three:** one **reliable damage** Surge in your primary stat, one **control** Surge (a rider that fits your trees), and one **Infuse** or **area** Surge for the team. Think ahead: at level 4 one Surge becomes your **always-ready Signature**, so the one you'd want every round is a good candidate.

### 8. Gear and Scrip
Take one **Starting Kit** ([Ch 8](08_Gear.md#starting-kits)). All include Light armor (Armor 1), GRAFT ID, radio, flashlight, 2 rations and 1 Damper. Then take **50 Scrip** (+100 Corporate Washout, +50 Face kit) and buy from [Gear](08_Gear.md) if you like.
- Weapons: **Damage = Rank × die + stat**; one Mod per weapon (100 Scrip). Natural Weapons can take a Mod; Spit and Mind Spike can't.
- You wear **one** armor (Light *or* Heavy), plus an optional Shield. **Armor max 4** (5 with Unbreakable, 6 for a Juggernaut with Siege Hide).
- **Upkeep:** 25 Scrip per Chrome tree (and per Smart-Link) each mission. GRAFT prepays 25 of your first mission's.

### 9. Fill In the Numbers

| Number | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | ⌈level ÷ 2⌉ (1 at level 1) |
| **Max HP** | 12 + 2 × Grit + 5 × (level − 1); Hide *Thick* +2 × level |
| **Armor** | Light 1 / Heavy 2, Shield +1, *Plating* +1 (+2 from Rank 3), *Siege Hide* +1; max 4 (5 / 6) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft; +10 *Quickened*, +10 *Spring Legs*, −5 Heavy armor (+5 *Hollow Bones*) |
| **Defenses** | Dodge = Moxie + Rank · Endure = Grit + Rank · Resist = Nerve + Rank |
| **Flux Limit** | 4 + Rank + Nerve, minimum 4; then −1 if Splice |
| **Attack** | stat + Rank; damage Rank × die + stat |
| **Sequence / Prime / Juice** | d4 / 2 / 1 (max 3) |

### 10. Trackers
Mark the boxes you'll use in play: **Flux** (up to your Limit; going over is an Overload), **Death marks** (3), **Breathers** (2 per Lockdown), **Last Gasp** (1 per Lockdown), **Juice** (start each session with 1). Later: **Signature Surges** and **Final Form** (1 per Lockdown).

### 11. Drive
Answer: *Why did you take the Mutagen? What do you want before the campaign ends? Who in Halcyon do you owe, or hate?* Give the GM at least one name.

## Leveling Up (1–10)
One mission = one level ([Ch 7](07_Advancement.md)). Each level, in order:
1. **+5 Max HP** (+2 more with *Thick*).
2. **+1 primary-tree node** (always; you choose which) **and +1 node in your secondary or tertiary tree**. Gates: Tier II needs level 4 + 3 nodes in the tree; an Apex needs level 7 + 6 other nodes in the tree, and only in your primary or secondary tree. From level 9 the primary pick overflows into secondary/tertiary.
3. **+1 Surge** into the next empty slot. You may also **rewrite** one Surge, or rebuild one Signature.
4. **Optional respec:** swap one node for another of the same pick type that you qualify for.
5. **At levels 4 and 7, Evolve** (checklist below).
6. Recalculate Rank, HP, defenses, Limit, weapon dice.

| Level | Rank | Stage | Nodes (prim + sec/tert + Evo) | Surges | Die | Wild | Prime | Signatures | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Base | 3 + 3 | 3 | d4 | 1 | 2 | — | 3 Knacks |
| 2 | 1 | Base | 4 + 4 | 4 | d6 | 2 | 2 | — | |
| 3 | 2 | Base | 5 + 5 | 5 | d6 | 1 | 2 | — | +1 Knack |
| **4** | 2 | **Evo I** | 6 + 6 + 1 | 6 | **d8** | 2 | **3** | **1** | **Evolution I**; Tier II |
| 5 | 3 | Evo I | 7 + 7 + 1 | 7 | d8 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 2 Twists; Plating +2 |
| 6 | 3 | Evo I | 8 + 8 + 1 | 8 | d8 | 0 | 3 | 1 | +1 Knack |
| **7** | 4 | **Evo II** | 9 + 9 + 2 | 9 | **d12** | 3 | **4** | **2** | **Evolution II**; Apex |
| 8 | 4 | Evo II | 10 + 10 + 2 | 10 | d12 | 2 | 4 | 2 | Primary full |
| 9 | 5 | Evo II | 10 + 12 + 2 | 11 | d12 | 1 | 4 | 2 | +1 Knack; overflow |
| 10 | 5 | Final | 10 + 14 + 2 | 12 | d12 | 0 | 4 | 2 | **Final Form** |

### The Evolution Checklist (levels 4 and 7)
1. **Stats:** Evo I → +5 / +3 / +2 / +1 / +0. Evo II → +8 / +4 / +3 / +2 / +1 (same order as your level 1 placement).
2. **Recalculate** HP (Grit changed), defenses, Flux Limit (Nerve changed), and every attack and Surge line.
3. **Prime** +1 (3, then 4). **Sequence die** up (d8, then d12): you'll have new Wild slots.
4. **Write your Form's Evolution node** into your primary tree ([Ch 3](03_Augment_Trees.md#the-evolution-nodes-at-a-glance)).
5. **Build a Signature Surge** and write it in a Signature box. It's always Primed; once per round; normal Flux.
6. **Describe your new look** (your Form's stage table in [Ch 2A](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md)), and plan your **Emergence** with the GM.

**Final Form (level 10):** once per Lockdown at the start of a combat: primary stat +2, Prime two extra, every Surge Amped.

- **Lab Escapee** has one fewer Wild slot (none at levels 6 and 10). It never adds Signatures.
- **In play** you may also gain: **Scars/Faults** from Overloads (some change numbers: *Hollow Bones*, *Drained Cell*, *Brittle Shell*, *Sticky Relay*), a **Blossom** Knack, *Patch Applied* (+1 Limit), **Injuries** until Lockdown, and **Samples** (a Surge spliced into a Wild slot for 100 Scrip; it may use the Boss's parts).
- **Scrip:** each mission pays 200 + 100 × level; pay Upkeep before the next one.

**Planning tip:** your primary tree fills by level 8 whatever you do, so the real choices are **order** (what you want before Evolution I) and **where your secondary/tertiary picks go**. Pour them into the secondary if you want its Apex at level 7–9 (you'll need 7 nodes there), or spread them for utility.

## Common Mistakes
- **Taking a node outside your Form's trees.** You only have three.
- **Wrong split at level 1.** It's 3 primary / 2 secondary / 1 tertiary, Roots first.
- **Skipping the primary pick.** You get one every level until the tree is full (level 8), and must take it.
- **Tier II before level 4**, or with fewer than 3 nodes in that tree.
- **An Apex in your tertiary tree**, before level 7, or with fewer than 6 other nodes in the tree.
- **Choosing your Evolution node.** It's fixed by your Form and comes free at 4 and 7.
- **Forgetting to recalculate at an Evolution:** HP (Grit), Limit (Nerve), every attack and Surge line.
- **Writing a Signature on your Sequence.** It goes in its own box and doesn't count toward Surges known.
- **Using a part you haven't unlocked.** Only base parts (Strike, Bolt, Infuse, Kinetic) and your Roots' unlocks.
- **Infuse with a damage Effect**, or Bolt with Mend. Infuse only takes Infuse Effects.
- **Wrong Twist:** Chain on a Burst, Linger on a Bolt, Overcharge on Infuse, two Twists before Rank 3, the same Twist twice.
- **Adding the stat to area damage.** Burst, Wave and Pulse roll dice only.
- **Forgetting Splice −1 Limit** or **Chrome Upkeep** (and disadvantage vs Shock).
- **Armor over the cap,** or wearing Light and Heavy together.
- **Knowing too many Surges.** Surges known = level + 2 (Lab Escapee +1, Samples extra); the rest are Wild, on purpose.

## Worked Example: Dree "Brownout" Ocampo (Siren)

### Level 1 — Siren
1. **Concept:** Ex-Ward electrician who wired her voice into the power grid. She's a pirate-radio preacher of the blackout who can't stop broadcasting.
2. **Form:** **Siren** (Chutzpah primary; Mind / Wiring / Limbs).
3. **Stats:** Chutzpah +3, Moxie +2, Gumption +1 (set by the Form). She puts **Nerve +0**, **Grit −1** (she's skinny, but steady around live wires).
4. **Origin:** Ward Militia: *Squad Tactics*; +2 on group initiative, Brace as Quick once per combat.
5. **Knacks:** *Squad Tactics*, *Electrical Repair*, *Rooftop Routes*.
6. **Augments (6):**
   - Mind (**Bio**): *Uplink* (Root), *Intrude*, *Commanding Voice*.
   - Wiring (**Chrome**): *Quickened* (Root), *Slipstream*.
   - Limbs (**Bio**): *Natural Weapons* (Root).

   She has both Materials, so she is a **Splice**. Unlocked: Dread, Link, Shock, Blaze, Haste, Wave, Snare.
7. **Sequence (d4, Prime 2):**

| # | Surge | Build | Flux | At level 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sizzle Line | Wave + Blaze (30-ft line; Moxie via Wiring) | 1 | +3 vs each Guard, 2d6 Blaze, Burning |
| 2 | Brownout | Bolt + Shock (Moxie via Wiring) | 1 | +3, 2d8+2 Shock, Dazed |
| 3 | Dead Air | Bolt + Dread (Chutzpah via Mind) | 1 | +4, 2d8+3 Psychic, Shaken |
| 4 | *Wild* | | | |

   Firmware can swap in *Sizzle Line* or *Brownout* (they use Blaze/Shock from her Chrome Wiring); *Dead Air* uses only Bio Mind parts, so it can't be swapped in.

8. **Gear:** Face kit (Concealed Pistol, Light melee, Hack deck, Flashbang, +50 Scrip) + common items. 100 Scrip; buys a Medkit (25), 75 left. Upkeep 25 for Wiring, prepaid for mission 1.
9. **Numbers:** Rank 1 · HP **10** · Armor **1** · Speed **40** · Dodge **+3**, Endure **+0**, Resist **+1** · Flux Limit **4** (4 + 1 + 0 = 5, −1 Splice) · Knit 2 · Juice 1.
   **Attacks:** Mind Spike +4, 1d8+3 Psychic (60 ft) · Claws +3, 1d8+2 · Knife +3, 1d6+2 · Pistol +1, 1d6.
   **Material:** Knit 2 HP (2 Bio trees × Rank 1); Firmware; Overload rolls both tables; disadvantage vs Shock.
10. **Drive:** She took the Mutagen to survive a substation fire. She wants the Ward's grid back on. She owes Scrapjack boss **Tallow** a new spine.

*Playing her at level 1:* her Limit of 4 is tiny, so two Surges and a Churn is an Overload. Lead with *Dead Air*, keep Commanding Voice (Quick Rally, +4 temporary HP to everyone) for the bad round, and stay behind the Juggernaut.

### Level 4 — Evolution I: Diva
Level-ups: **2** *Iron Will* · *Wall Run* · Surge *Wire Up* (Infuse + Haste). **3** *Read the Room* · *Spring Legs* · Surge *Static Snare* (Wave + Snare) · Knack *Pirate Radio*. **4** *Psychic Scream* (Tier II) · *Overclock* (Tier II; Wiring has 3 nodes) · Surge *Overvolt* (Bolt + Shock + Overcharge) · **Evolution I**.
- **Look (Splice Diva):** iridescent skin and a frill of fins at the neck (Bio Mind); speaker grilles bloom along her collarbones and hum with mains current (Chrome Wiring).
- **Stats:** Chutzpah **+5**, Moxie +3, Gumption +2, Nerve +1, Grit +0. **Rank 2.**
- **Nodes (13):**
  - Mind (Bio): Uplink, Intrude, Commanding Voice, Iron Will, Read the Room, Psychic Scream, plus the Evolution node **Siren Song**.
  - Wiring (Chrome): Quickened, Slipstream, Wall Run, Overclock.
  - Limbs (Bio): Natural Weapons, Spring Legs.
- **Numbers:** HP **27** (12 + 0 + 15) · Armor 1 · Speed **50** · Dodge **+5**, Endure **+2**, Resist **+3** (advantage; can't be Shaken) · Flux Limit **6** (4 + 2 + 1 − 1) · Knit 4 · Prime **3** · Sequence **d8**.
- **Attacks:** Mind Spike +7, 2d8+5 Psychic · Claws +5, 2d8+3.
- **Signature Surge:** ***The Broadcast*** (Wave + Dread, Chutzpah): +7 vs each Guard in a 15-ft cone or 30-ft line, 3d6 Psychic, Shaken. 1 Flux, once per round, always ready.
- **Sequence (d8, Prime 3):**

| # | Surge | Build | Flux | At level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sizzle Line | Wave + Blaze | 1 | +5, 3d6, Burning (4/turn) |
| 2 | Brownout | Bolt + Shock | 1 | +5, 3d8+3, Dazed |
| 3 | Dead Air | Bolt + Dread | 1 | +7, 3d8+5, Shaken |
| 4 | Wire Up | Infuse + Haste | 1 | Ally takes a Move + Action, advantage |
| 5 | Static Snare | Wave + Snare (Moxie) | 1 | +5, 3d6, Held |
| 6 | Overvolt | Bolt + Shock + Overcharge | 2 | +5, 5d8+3, Dazed |
| 7–8 | *Wild* | | | |

- **Emergence** (first fight of mission 4): she gains 8 temporary HP, every enemy within 30 ft is Shaken, and her first *Broadcast* costs 0 Flux.
- *What changed:* her primary attack went from +4 to **+7** against a T4 Guard of 14 (a 70% hit chance; at level 3 she had +5 against Guard 14, 60%). She can Surge every round, and Siren Song sends whole rooms at Rook. Her Limit of 6 is still her weak point. Every turn she Surges costs 1 Flux, so Surging every turn Overloads her partway through the second fight before a Breather. Mix in Mind Spike turns.

### Level 7 — Evolution II: Choir
Level-ups: **5** *Puppet* · *Afterimage* · Surge *Crowd Surge* (Infuse + Link + Sure). **6** *Glamour* · *Grapnel* · Surge *Arc Halo* (Wave + Shock + Reach) · Knack *Crowd Work*. **7** **Apex *Hive Mind*** (level 7, and 8 other Mind nodes) · *Arc Jump* · Surge *Blackout* (Bolt + Dread + Chain + Overcharge) · **Evolution II**.
- **Look (Splice Choir):** a crown of soft horns and a dozen small mouths singing along her arms (Bio); an antenna crown and a cape of fibre-optic cable that broadcasts on every frequency (Chrome).
- **Stats:** Chutzpah **+8**, Moxie +4, Gumption +3, Nerve +2, Grit +1. **Rank 4.**
- **Nodes (20):**
  - Mind (Bio): Uplink, Intrude, Commanding Voice, Iron Will, Read the Room, Psychic Scream, Puppet, Glamour, **Hive Mind**, plus **Siren Song** and **Crown of Will**.
  - Wiring (Chrome): Quickened, Slipstream, Wall Run, Overclock, Afterimage, Arc Jump.
  - Limbs (Bio): Natural Weapons, Spring Legs, Grapnel.
- **Numbers:** HP **44** (12 + 2 + 30) · Armor 1 · Speed 50 · Dodge **+8**, Endure **+5**, Resist **+6** · Flux Limit **9** (4 + 4 + 2 − 1) · Knit 8 · Prime **4** · Sequence **d12** (9 Surges + 3 Wild).
- **Attacks:** Mind Spike +12, 4d8+8 Psychic · Claws +8, 4d8+4.
- **Signature Surges (2):** *The Broadcast*, now +12, 5d6 Psychic, Shaken, and ***Live Feed*** (Infuse + Haste): every round, an ally takes a Move and an Action with advantage. Each costs 1 Flux, once per round. She can't use both in one turn (one Action), unless *Overclock* gives her a second Action.
- **New Sequence entries:** 7 *Crowd Surge* (2 Flux: target + 4 allies + 1 extra get advantage on their next roll) · 8 *Arc Halo* (2 Flux: +8, 5d6 Shock, Dazed, 30-ft cone or 60-ft line) · 9 *Blackout* (3 Flux: +12, 7d8+8 Psychic, Shaken, then half damage to a second target within 15 ft) · 10–12 *Wild*.
- *What changed:* +12 against a T7 Guard of 17 is an **80%** hit. *Crown of Will* turns the enemy's best Soldier into hers for the fight. *Hive Mind* lets an ally borrow her +8 Chutzpah for one roll a round, and gives one ally an extra Prime. She is the party's engine now, but with Dodge +8 against TN 16 she still fails 35% of the time. She stays out of reach and lets Siren Song point the room at the tank.

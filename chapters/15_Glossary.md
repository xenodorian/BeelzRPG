# 15. Glossary

Every game term in one place, A to Z, with where its full rule lives.

| Term | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Action** | The main thing you do on your turn (Attack, Unleash, Dash, Brace, Help, Patch, Analyze, Taunt, Rally, Grapple, Shove…) | [Actions](06_Combat.md#actions) |
| **Advantage / Disadvantage** | Roll 2d20, keep the higher / lower. They don't stack; both cancel | [Core Rules](01_Core_Rules.md#advantage-and-disadvantage) |
| **Amped** | A Primed Surge rolled as doubles (or made so by Emergence or Final Form): it costs 0 Flux | [Priming](04_Surges.md#priming) |
| **Analyze** | Gumption action on a creature you can see within 60 ft: learn its stats, and it's Exposed | [Actions](06_Combat.md#actions) |
| **Apex** | A tree's capstone node: level 7+, 6 other nodes in the tree, primary or secondary tree only | [How Trees Work](03_Augment_Trees.md#how-trees-work) |
| **Area / Single** | Attack types. An Area still deals half damage on a miss or a successful defense | [Attacking](06_Combat.md#attacking), [Areas](06_Combat.md#areas-both-ways) |
| **Armor** | Flat reduction to each hit (minimum 1 damage); max 4 (5 with Unbreakable, 6 with Siege Hide) | [Damage](06_Combat.md#damage), [Armor](08_Gear.md#armor) |
| **Augment / Node / Root / Tree** | Your skill-tree abilities. A Root opens a tree; each tree has 10 standard nodes | [Augment Trees](03_Augment_Trees.md) |
| **Base Form** | One of five body plans (Juggernaut, Skitter, Oracle, Alembic, Siren): sets your stats and your primary, secondary and tertiary trees | [Base Forms](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md) |
| **Bio / Chrome / Splice** | A tree's **Material**: grown or installed. A Splice has both | [Material](03_Augment_Trees.md#material-bio-or-chrome) |
| **Bloom / Glitch** | The Overload tables for Bio / Chrome | [Overload](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#overload) |
| **Boss** | A centrepiece enemy: sheds a condition at the start of its turn, gets one Interrupt per round | [Role](10_Threats.md#3-role) |
| **Boss Rating (BR) / Minion Rating (MR)** | The party level at which one creature (BR) or a group worth 1 unit each (MR) is a fair fight for 4 rested freelancers | [Ratings](10_Threats.md#1-ratings-br-and-mr) |
| **Brace** | Action: defenses with advantage until your next turn | [Actions](06_Combat.md#actions) |
| **Breather / Lockdown** | Short rest (half Max HP, Flux 0, two per Lockdown) / full rest (everything resets) | [Rest](01_Core_Rules.md#rest) |
| **Budget / Units** | Encounter currency: a Hard (fair) fight is 1 unit per freelancer; each creature's units come from its rating minus the party level | [Building an Encounter](10_Threats.md#5-building-an-encounter) |
| **Churn** | Quick action: +1 Flux, roll your Sequence die once more. Not on a turn you Unleash | [Churning](04_Surges.md#churning) |
| **Close / Near / Far / Distant** | Theatre-of-the-mind distances (5 / 30 / 120 ft / beyond) | [Positioning](06_Combat.md#positioning-theatre-of-the-mind-or-grid) |
| **Conditions** | Blinded, Burning, Dazed, Exposed, Held, Poisoned, Prone, Shaken, Veiled; most last until the end of the affected creature's next turn | [Conditions](06_Combat.md#conditions) |
| **Crit** | A natural 20 on an attack: roll the damage dice twice | [Natural 20s](01_Core_Rules.md#natural-20s-and-1s) |
| **Death mark** | Gained while Down; 3 means dead | [Going Down](06_Combat.md#going-down) |
| **Defenses** | Dodge (Moxie), Endure (Grit), Resist (Nerve), each + Rank. Players roll them against an enemy's TN | [Defending](06_Combat.md#defending) |
| **Down** | At 0 HP: you can't act, you roll to stabilise, damage gives death marks | [Going Down](06_Combat.md#going-down) |
| **Effect / Shape / Twist** | The parts you build a Surge from | [Building a Surge](04_Surges.md#building-a-surge) |
| **Emergence** | The on-screen moment of an Evolution: temp HP 2 × level, enemies within 30 ft who see it are Shaken, first Signature use Amped | [Emergence](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md#emergence-the-moment-it-happens) |
| **Engulf** | A Heavy's Area deals extra damage for each freelancer caught beyond the first | [Traits](10_Threats.md#4-traits) |
| **Enemy Phase / Player Phase** | The two halves of a round: freelancers act in any order, then the enemies act one by one | [The Round](06_Combat.md#the-round) |
| **Evolution (I / II)** | The metamorphosis at level 4 / 7: new stats, +1 Prime, a bigger Sequence die, an Evolution node, a Signature Surge, a new look | [What an Evolution Gives](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md#what-an-evolution-gives) |
| **Exposed** | A condition: −2 Guard (enemies) or −2 to defense rolls (freelancers) | [Conditions](06_Combat.md#conditions) |
| **Fair fight** | Won ≥ 90% of the time, someone Down 40–60%, a death ≤ 5%, 3–7 rounds (a Hard fight) | [Ratings](10_Threats.md#1-ratings-br-and-mr) |
| **Final Form** | Level 10: once per Lockdown, +2 primary stat, two extra Primes, every Surge Amped for a fight | [Final Form](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md#final-form-level-10) |
| **Firmware / Knit** | The Chrome / Bio Material perks (swap a Surge / heal after fights) | [Material](03_Augment_Trees.md#material-bio-or-chrome) |
| **Flux / Flux Limit** | Instability from Surges; Limit = 4 + Rank + Nerve. Going over it is an Overload | [Flux](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#flux) |
| **GRAFT** | The contractor that hires the freelancers | [The Setting](00_Introduction.md#the-setting-in-one-page) |
| **Grid** | Playing on a battle map: 1 square = 5 ft, diagonals count 1 | [On a Grid](06_Combat.md#on-a-grid) |
| **Guard** | The number freelancers attack against | [Threat Level](10_Threats.md#2-threat-level) |
| **Heavy / Pack** | Enemy kinds: a Heavy has a big Area attack (costs ×1¼ units on a grid); a Pack enemy has group Traits | [Role](10_Threats.md#3-role), [Building an Encounter](10_Threats.md#5-building-an-encounter) |
| **Injury** | Temporary harm when you get up from Down (d6) | [Injury Table](06_Combat.md#injury-table-d6) |
| **Interrupt** | A Boss's one extra action (no Move) after any freelancer's turn, once per round | [The Round](06_Combat.md#the-round) |
| **Juice** | Reward token (max 3): reroll a d20, choose a Prime, or Shrug a condition | [Juice](01_Core_Rules.md#juice) |
| **Knack** | A narrow specialty; add Rank to checks that use it | [Knacks](01_Core_Rules.md#knacks) |
| **Last Gasp** | Once per Lockdown, when you'd drop to 0 HP: Overload on purpose instead and stay at 1 HP | [Last Gasp](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#last-gasp) |
| **Line of sight** | A clear line from you to the target; walls block it, creatures don't | [Ranges](06_Combat.md#ranges-both-ways) |
| **Long range** | Up to twice a ranged weapon's range, at disadvantage. Not for Areas, grenades or Surges | [Ranges](06_Combat.md#ranges-both-ways) |
| **Mook** | The weakest enemy Role: a quarter of the HP, half the damage, fielded in numbers | [Role](10_Threats.md#3-role) |
| **Move / Dash / Move carefully** | Your speed in feet each turn (split freely) / an Action for a second Move / half speed without provoking | [Your Turn](06_Combat.md#your-turn), [Opportunity Strikes](06_Combat.md#opportunity-strikes) |
| **Mutagenic X** | Enemy Trait: you gain X Flux when you fail your defense against its attack | [Mutagenic Threats](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#mutagenic-threats) |
| **Opportunity Strike** | A melee attack (using your Reaction) when an enemy leaves your reach; enemies get one per round each | [Opportunity Strikes](06_Combat.md#opportunity-strikes) |
| **Origin** | Your background: a Knack and a Perk | [Origins](02_Character_Creation.md#origins-roll-d6-or-choose) |
| **Overload** | Going over your Flux Limit: damage, a table roll, Flux back to 0 | [Overload](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#overload) |
| **Prime / Primed** | How many Surges you roll ready at the start of a fight (2; 3 from L4; 4 from L7), and a Surge that's ready | [Priming](04_Surges.md#priming) |
| **Primary / Secondary / Tertiary** | Your Form's three best stats (+3/+2/+1) and its three trees | [Base Forms](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md#choosing-a-form) |
| **Push** | Gain 1 Flux for a node's extra effect | [How Trees Work](03_Augment_Trees.md#how-trees-work) |
| **Quick action** | A small extra on your turn (draw, stim, Churn, Target Lock, talk) | [Quick Actions](06_Combat.md#quick-actions) |
| **Rank** | ⌈level ÷ 2⌉ (1–5): added to attacks, defenses and Knack checks; multiplies weapon dice | [The Roll](01_Core_Rules.md#the-roll) |
| **Reaction** | One per round, on anyone's turn: Opportunity Strikes and Reaction nodes | [Reactions](06_Combat.md#reactions) |
| **Reach** | The squares next to you (5 ft); some nodes and creatures reach 10 ft | [Opportunity Strikes](06_Combat.md#opportunity-strikes) |
| **Role / Trait / T** | The parts of a threat: its Threat level, its Role and its Traits | [Threats](10_Threats.md) |
| **Sample** | A harvested Boss or Elite part, spliced in as a Surge | [Samples](07_Advancement.md#samples) |
| **Scar / Fault** | Permanent Bio / Chrome Overload quirk | [Scars and Faults](05_Flux_and_Overload.md#scars-and-faults-d6) |
| **Scrip** | Money | [Gear](08_Gear.md) |
| **Sequence / Sequence die** | Your numbered Surge list, rolled with a d4 / d6 / d8 / d12; empty slots are Wild | [The Sequence](04_Surges.md#the-sequence) |
| **Signature Surge** | Always ready (once per round), one per Evolution; not on the Sequence | [Signature Surges](04_Surges.md#signature-surges) |
| **Spill / Spill Clock** | The disaster that made Halcyon / the GM's six-segment mission timer | [The Setting](00_Introduction.md#the-setting-in-one-page), [The Spill Clock](09_Running_the_Game.md#the-spill-clock) |
| **Stage** | Base, Evolution I, Evolution II, Final | [Stages](02A_Forms_and_Evolution.md#stages) |
| **Stats** | Grit, Moxie, Nerve, Gumption, Chutzpah; set by Form and stage | [The Five Stats](02_Character_Creation.md#the-five-stats) |
| **Surge / Unleash** | A random combat power / the Action that uses one | [Surges](04_Surges.md) |
| **Theatre of the mind** | Playing without a map, using the four distance bands | [Positioning](06_Combat.md#positioning-theatre-of-the-mind-or-grid) |
| **Tier I / Tier II** | Node tiers: Tier I needs the Root; Tier II needs level 4+ and 3 nodes in the tree | [How Trees Work](03_Augment_Trees.md#how-trees-work) |
| **TN (Threat Number)** | The number freelancers defend against | [Threat Level](10_Threats.md#2-threat-level) |
| **Upkeep** | 25 Scrip per Chrome tree per mission | [Material](03_Augment_Trees.md#material-bio-or-chrome) |
| **Wait** | Take your turn later in the Player Phase | [The Round](06_Combat.md#the-round) |
| **Wild / Wild Surge** | An empty Sequence slot; roll on the d12 Wild table | [Wild Surge Table](04_Surges.md#wild-surge-table-d12) |
